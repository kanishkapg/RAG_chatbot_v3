CENTRALIZED VEHICLE POOL (CVP) AT SLT HEADQUARTERS

The management has decided to establish a centralized vehicle pool at SLT Headquarters with effect from 19th October 2020 to optimize the usage of SLT vehicles currently assigned to different groups and also to effectively manage the cost on parking at the hired parking spaces. Most of the occasions in the day time, there are over 60 vehicles parked at these parking spaces continuously generating unnecessary cost to SLT whilst payments are made for hired vehicles and taxi services as well.

Guidelines;

1. A CVP, comprising of all the vehicles shall be operated common to all business units/cost centers at the SLT Headquarters in a single location irrespective of the line management hierarchy of each business unit. The ownership of operating such a vehicle pool shall be vested on CAO.

2. The vehicle request shall be submitted through SLT vehicle request management system. Distance travelled in a journey (up and down) is to be justified in the system as follows.
   i. Total distance up to 300km should be justified and recommended by A5 or above.
   ii. Total distance above 300km should be justified and recommended by DGM or above

3. Existing practice for taxi services will be continued on requirement basis subjected to availability of vehicles in CVP.

4. Monthly detailed system generated report on the usage of vehicles by units (pool vehicle usage, taxi services, etc.) will be issued to relevant DGM/GM/CXO for their review.

5. Required no of vehicles for operational work at night time will be made available and assigned to relevant groups from 5.00pm to 9.00am on following day. Requests for vehicle requirements for the morning must be made before 4.30 pm on the previous day.
