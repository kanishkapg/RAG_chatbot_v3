GUIDELINES FOR PREVENTION FROM NOVEL CORONAVIRUS (2019 –NCOV)

As you are aware, the world is facing a fast moving infection started from Chinese city of Wuhan
and then spread to number of other countries, which causes phenomena like symptoms, and
accordingly the World Health Organization (WHO) has declared a global emergency to strengthen
public health measures in other countries.
In the view of ensuring our employees' health & safety and introducing measures to halt the virus's
spread, the following guidelines will be introduced with immediate effect.

- Accordingly all the official foreign visits of our employees will be suspended until further
notice. However approvals will be granted only for the foreign visits on critical business
requirements subject to strong justification of business case.

- Further you are requested to refrain from travelling overseas for your personal
requirements unless the visit is mandatory until the prevailing situation is normalized.
However in a situation where your overseas visit is unavoidable, CEO's special prior
approval should be obtained.
For such requirement please contact Mr Hasitha Sandanayake, Manager – Health & Safety
on following contacts;
Mobile: 0715360869, Direct:  0112431220, e-mail: hasitha@slt.com.lk

- In order to reduce your risk of Coronavirus infection from outsiders, restrictions have been
imposed for foreigners on visiting SLT premises including regional SLT premises.
Arrangement have been made for the foreign vendors to get their technical support online
for urgent operational & maintenance activities in line with the company information
security procedures.

- Similar restriction will also be applicable for local visitors as well including contractor
staff/suppliers etc. All employees are requested to limit all non-critical visitors to their
premises (Excluding customer touch points). However for visitors of critical business
requirement, prior approval should be obtained from relevant GM-SLT by the user
department.

- Further the SLT management is advising employees to follow the standard procedures (in Sinhalese) issued by Epidemiology Unit under the Ministry of Health to reduce the chance of infecting by the virus.
