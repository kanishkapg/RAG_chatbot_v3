OPTIMIZING THE STOCK HOLDING TIME

It is observed that SLT has tied up its funds holding heavy inventory for longer durations which may have a direct impact on company’s bottom-line as well as limit investments on projects with attractive returns.

To address this issue by optimizing the stock holding times, following guidelines are issued:

1 At present there are weekly deliveries to all Provincial Stores except to Provincial Stores in North & East. The deliveries to Provincial Stores in North and East are scheduled once in two weeks.
Hence, when ordering material from Main Stores to Provincial Stores through IR, the Stores Officers originating IR and DGMM who authorize the IR shall ensure the total material availability in the Province after receiving goods shall not exceed 150% of the requirement for the period between two deliveries.

2 When obtaining material from Provincial Stores through Move Orders Sub Inventory Owners shall ensure not to exceed material availability of Sub Inventory (inventory of its all locators) 150% of the requirement between two deliveries from Provincial Stores.

3 When ordering material from Suppliers for a project, the quantity shall not exceed three month’s requirement.

4 When handing over the material to contractors, maximum of one month’s requirement shall be handed over. If a contractor retains the goods for a period more than three months without installation & invoicing, for each calendar month 10% of the cost of material retained shall be set off as the penalty from the invoices payable. This is applicable for material issued from 1st August 2019.

5 The Project Managers are responsible and accountable to reconcile the material issued against the installations and get the items which had been kept with the contractor for more than 6 months returned to main stores.

6 Here after, all the material retained in Provincial Stores/Special Stores & Locators for more than 6 months other than spares need to be returned to Peliyagoda or Kotugoda Stores from where they were issued.
7 All the stocks held in any type of Store including Contractor Stores and Locators for more than 6 month- period to 1st July 2019 except spares need to be returned on or before 31.07. 2019.

This circular is enforced with immediate effect and compliance is expected from all the parties to achieve a lean supply chain by the end of Material Management Month, i.e. end July 2019.
