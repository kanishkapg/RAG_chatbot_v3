DRIVING TOWARDS PERFORMANCE BASED ORGANIZATIONAL CULTURE

In view of enhancing the performance evaluation management system, we started introducing various improvements to the existing performance management system. The improvements will create a rewarding culture and encourage employees who are contributing positively to the growth of the organization. The management intends that the revised system will encourage the staff to drive the annual business targets and to achieve objectives of the organization more effectively while delivering higher level of customer satisfaction.

The improved Performance Management System will introduce KPI based rewarding scheme, peer evaluation mechanism, continuous follow up and feedback and Training & Development needs of the employee. Employee performances for the year will be reviewed against achievement of assigned KPI, which will be monitored carefully and reviewed throughout the year. The evaluations will be conducted by a team of supervising officers and peers of the respective supervising officer in order to ensure a fair and transparent evaluation.

Employees will receive feedback on their performance as well as opportunities for improvements and growth. The evaluation process is conducted with the employee's career development at SLT in mind as the process supports the identification of Training & Development needs of the employee as well. Accordingly, employees will also be given Training opportunities in the subsequent year to address the identified knowledge and skill gaps.

This improved Performance Management System and following evaluation mechanism will be implemented effective from year 2021 performance management cycle (Evaluation period 1st Jan 2021 - 31st Dec 2021) and onwards.

A. KPI based Rewarding Scheme:

Annual Business Plan based Targets for the year 2021 have been already defined and those targets are cascaded from CXO up to the lower level staff of each CXO group and communicated to respective staff through their reporting hierarchy.

A.1 Corporate Level KPIs

Corporate Level KPIs have been defined by the Corporate Office based on the expected Turnover, EBITDA, PBT for the year concerned and given them as Company KPIs for all staff levels (i.e. executive and non-executive staff) which will be applicable with a weightage to each level as shown in the Table 1 below.

| Staff Level    | Weightage of Company KPIs |
|---|---|
| CXO    | 40%    |
| GM    | 30%    |
| DGM    | 25%    |
| Other Executives | 20%    |
| Non-Executives | 15%    |

Table 01: Corporate Level KPIs and Weightage
