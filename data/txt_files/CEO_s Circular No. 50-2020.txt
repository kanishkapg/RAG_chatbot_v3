POSTS OF GENERAL MANAGER

| VII    | GM-Marketing Services    | CMO    | Engineering / Marketing    |

Applications are invited from the permanent Deputy General Managers of A2, A3 and A4 grades of SLT, who satisfy the under mentioned requirements to be considered for the above posts.

1. ELIGIBILITY

1.1 FOR OFFICERS PRESENTLY IN A3 GRADE:

(a) Engineering Profession
(i) Corporate Membership of IESL/IET (IEE) UK/IMech E(UK)/Institution of Civil Engineers (UK) with 5 years' experience in A3 Grade in SLT

(b) Accounting Profession
(i) Associate Membership of ICA/CIMA/ACCA with 5 years' experience in A3 Grade in SLT

(c) Human Resources Profession
(i) Degree of a recognized university with full membership of CIPM (IPM) Sri Lanka or equivalent with 5 years' experience in A3 Grade in SLT

(d) Marketing Profession
(i) Membership (MCIM) of CIM or equivalent qualification with 5 years' experience in A3 Grade in SLT

(e) Legal Profession
(i) An Attorney at law and Notary Public with 5 years' experience in A3 Grade in SLT

1.2 FOR OFFICERS PRESENTLY IN A4 GRADE:

(a) Engineering Profession
(i) Corporate Membership of IESL/IET (IEE) UK/ IMech E(UK)/Institution of Civil Engineers(UK) with 3 years engineering experience in A4 Grade in SLT OR
(ii) An Engineering Degree/Associate Membership of IESL/IET (IEE) UK/IMech E(UK)/Institution of Civil Engineers(UK) with 5 years engineering experience in A4 Grade in SLT OR
(iii) 7 years engineering experience in A4 Grade in SLT

(b) Accounting Profession
(i) Associate Membership of ICA/CIMA/ACCA with 3 years accounting / auditing experience in A4 Grade in SLT OR
(ii) Intermediate qualification of ICA/CIMA/ACCA with 5 years accounting / auditing experience in A4 Grade in SLT OR
(iii) 7 years accounting / auditing experience in A4 Grade in SLT

(c) Human Resources Profession
(i) Degree of a recognized university with full membership of CIPM (IPM) Sri Lanka or equivalent with 3 years' experience in A4 Grade in SLT OR
(ii) A Degree of a recognized university or associate membership of CIPM (IPM) or equivalent with 5 years' experience in A4 Grade in SLT OR
(iii) 7 years' experience in A4 Grade in SLT

(d) Marketing Profession
(i) Membership (MCIM) of CIM or equivalent qualification with 3 years' experience in A4 Grade in SLT OR
(ii) Associate Graduate Membership(ACIM) of CIM or equivalent qualification with 5 years' experience in marketing in A4 Grade in SLT OR
(iii) 7 years marketing experience in A4 Grade in SLT

(e) Legal Profession
(i) An Attorney at law and Notary Public with 5 years' experience in A4 Grade in SLT

1.3 Deputy General Managers in A3 Grade who do not possess required number of years' experience in A3 Grade under category 1.1 will be eligible under category 1.2 by adding the number of years' experience in A4 Grade and A3 Grade together and those officers may also apply.

2. OPPORTUNITY FOR PRESENT GMs & DGMs IN A2 GRADE
GMs (A2/A3) and DGMs (A2) who wish to be considered for the above positions may also apply.

3. SELECTION METHOD
Selection will be by an interview.

4. PLACEMENT
Selected candidate will be initially appointed in Acting capacity. Subsequent to serving in the acting capacity for 06 months' period, if the selected officer is in A3 Grade, he /she will be placed in A2 Grade and if the selected officer is in A4 Grade he / she will be placed in A3 Grade. Further, if the selected officer is in A2 Grade, his/her Grade will remain unchanged.

5. METHOD OF APPLYING
Eligible candidates should fill in the attached application form and forward to GM-HR & IR (cover up) through the relevant Supervising Officer.

6. CLOSING DATE
Applications should be sent within 07 days of issuing this circular.
