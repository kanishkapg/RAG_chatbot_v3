ESTABLISHING INDIVIDUAL TARGETS & OBJECTIVES - 2019

I am pleased to inform you that the starting of establishing annual individual targets & objectives for year 2019 should be commenced with immediate effect and the effective duration will be from 01st January 2019 to 31st December 2019.

Please note that all supervising officers are responsible on ensuring establishing individual targets for their subordinates in line with the company ABP. Therefore, all executive supervising officers are requested to ensure that the correct establishment of targets for their subordinates. Duly completed targets/objectives should be updated in the ERP system by the employee for A10 and above grades. Completed individual target forms for the other staff should be kept under the custodian of the relevant Manager, DGM or above officer for any future reference and these individual target forms should be made available for auditing and for management reference at any time. All targets should be clearly communicated to the employees.

Establishing Individual Targets
Individual targets of all A10 and above staff (A1 to A10) should be set-up through ERP system (they can update the ERP system once 2019 SLT Performance Management Plan is published through ERP). Setting targets for A11 and below (A11 to A16) staff should be done using manual form.

Target Setting Guidelines

Accordingly following documents can be downloaded through HR website in the intranet.

- Performance management policy & process.
- Target setting form applicable to A11 and below staff.
- Target setting ERP user guide.

Please read the complete policy document and ERP user manual prior to commencement of the target setting process. All executive supervising officers are requested to educate their subordinates on the performance appraisal policy document which include target setting guidelines and to facilitate the creating individual KPIs. Guidelines and other material can be downloaded from

- **Intranet>Group>CHRO>Human Resource>HR Strategies & Policy> Performance Management>Target & Objective.**

The following directions will guide you in establishing, achieving and monitoring targets & objectives for your staff:

- All Chief Officers are requested to establish individual targets and objectives for their subordinates in line with Group/Divisional operational plan based on the company Annual Business Plan (ABP). Accordingly group’s ABP targets should be mapped into General Manager, Deputy General Manager and all staff reporting below them.

- Key Results Areas (KRAs)/Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) recommended by the Management/ABP for each group may be taken into consideration when establishing the objectives/targets.

- All supervising officers are responsible to establish targets to all their subordinates. Supervising officers also should ensure update their subordinates’ targets in ERP system and completion of manual targets forms before closing date.

- Chairman and Chief Officers will peer audit DGM and above officers to ensure cross functional alignment of ABP objectives with operational plans and staff responsibilities on targets & objectives.

- Targets & Objectives must be defined considering the short term and long term goals of the group and they should be defined based on ABP 2019 targets and respective divisional & sectional operational plans should be linked to ABP 2019 targets.

- Individual targets have to be SMART and linked to operational plan targets.
  - Specific (no ambiguity)
  - Measurable (in terms of time, number, volume or value)
  - Agreed (mutually agreed)
  - Realistic (can be particularly attainable, but only with an extra effort)
  - Time- bound (must have a time schedule)

- They should be defined as appropriate for each employee under the following key areas.
  - Customer
  - Financial
  - Internal Business Processes
  - Learning & Growth

- Achievement of individual targets should be monitored monthly by all reporting officers. Employees should discuss their targets and progress with their reporting officers regularly (at least every month). These documents should be made available for auditing and for management reference at any time.

- Reviewing Officers should monitor the progress of performance throughout the year. Progress and deviations (if any) should be monitored continuously and corrective measures should be taken with the help of respective reporting officers.

- Employees are not permitted to add, modify or omit targets once they are approved by the relevant authority. Any changes should be done with instructions/guidance and proper approvals of the respective supervising officer and the reviewer.

- Closing date for completion is on or before 15th February 2019.
