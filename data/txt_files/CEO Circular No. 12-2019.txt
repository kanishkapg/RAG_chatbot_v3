Approval for leaving the Workplace

It was observed that some employees used to leave their workplace and enter into other workplaces in different locations without any prior approval from their supervising officers. This practice appears inappropriate and in fact affected day to day operations significantly.

All employees are hereby advised to adhere to following guidelines with immediate effect;

1. Prior approval from CXO/DCXO/GMM/DGMM/RTOMM/M-OPMC should be obtained before leaving the workplace and entering other work places for duties or any other reason.

2. Prior approval from Regional GM should be obtained before entering into any SLT offices in regions by trade union members/leaders or any group of employees.

All employees are required to abide by the above instructions. Any deviations will result in stern disciplinary action being taken against such employees.

All CXO/DCXO/GMM/DGMM/RTOMM/M-OPMC are responsible that the staff under their purview will follow the above instructions.

K. A. Kithuri Perera  
Chief Executive Officer
