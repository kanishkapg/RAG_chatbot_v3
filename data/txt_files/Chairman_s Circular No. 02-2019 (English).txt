Chairman’s Circular No. 02/2019

Chairman’s Office
Sri Lanka Telecom PLC
Colombo 01.

2019/01/22

To : All SLT Employees,

PROPOSED MOTIVATIONAL INTERIM ADVANCE PAYMENT BASED ON PERFORMANCE FOR THE YEAR 2019

In view of encouraging and obtaining overall commitment of the employees to achieve the corporate targets for the year 2019, the Board of Directors has decided to make a performance based interim advance payment of Rs.25,000.00 as a token to all staff.

This payment will be provisioned according to the performance based on weighted salary index for 2019.

We are pleased to inform you that this payment will be made on the 01st of February 2019.

P.G. Kumarasinghe Sirisena
Chairman
