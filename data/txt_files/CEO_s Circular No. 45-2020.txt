CEO's Office, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 01.

To All Executive Staff,

POSTS OF DEPUTY GENERAL MANAGER

SLT Services Pvt Ltd (SLTS) is a SLT subsidiary company operated under SLT Group. To streamline the operations as per SLT strategic priorities, following vacancies are advertised internal to SLT PLC.

Applications are invited from the permanent executive staff of A3, A4 and A5 grades of SLT, who satisfy the under mentioned requirements and selected candidates will be promoted to Deputy General Manager positions as per the provisions available in the SRPS and thereafter will be seconded to SLTS for below mentioned positions.

1. DGM - System Integration

2. DGM - OSP

1. ELIGIBILITY

1.1 FOR OFFICERS PRESENTLY IN A4 GRADE:

(a) Engineering Profession

(i) Corporate Membership of IESL/IEE(UK)/IMechE(UK)/Institution of Civil Engineers(UK) WITH 03 years' experience in A4 Grade in SLT OR
(ii) An Engineering Degree / Associate Membership of IESL / IEE(UK) / IMechE(UK) / Institution of Civil Engineers(UK) WITH 05 years' experience in A4 Grade in SLT OR
(iii) 7 years' engineering experience in A4 Grade in SLT

1.2 FOR OFFICERS PRESENTLY IN A5 GRADE:

(a) Engineering Profession

(i) Corporate Membership of IESL/IEE(UK)/IMechE(UK)/Institution of Civil Engineers(UK) WITH 03 years' experience in A5 Grade in SLT OR
(ii) An Engineering Degree / Associate Membership of IESL / IEE(UK) / IMechE(UK) / Institution of Civil Engineers(UK) WITH 06 years' experience in A5 Grade in SLT OR
(iii) 08 years' engineering experience in A5 Grade in SLT

1.3 Officers in A4 Grade who do not possess required number of experience in A4 Grade under category 1.1 will be eligible under category 1.2 by adding the number of years' experience in A5 Grade and A4 Grade together and those officers may also apply.

2. OPPORTUNITY FOR PRESENT DGMs & OTHER OFFICERS IN A3 GRADE
    DGMs (A3/A4) or any other A3 grade officers in engineering profession who wish to be considered for the above positions may also apply.

3. SELECTION METHOD
    Selection will be by an interview.

4. PLACEMENT
    Selected candidate will be initially appointed in Acting capacity. Subsequent to serving in the acting capacity for 06 months' period, if the selected officer is in A4 Grade, he/she will be placed in A3 Grade and if the selected officer is in A5 Grade he/she will be placed in A4 Grade. Further, if the selected officer is in A3 Grade, his/her Grade remain unchanged.

5. METHOD OF APPLYING
    Eligible candidates should fill in the attached application form and forward to DGM- HR Operations through the relevant Supervising Officer.

6. CLOSING DATE
    Applications should be sent within 07 days of issuing this circular.

K A Kiththi Pepera
