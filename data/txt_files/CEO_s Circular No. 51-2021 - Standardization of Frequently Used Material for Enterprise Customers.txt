CEO's Office, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 01.

26 October 2021

To: All Employees,

STANDARDIZATION OF FREQUENTLY USED MATERIAL FOR ENTERPRISE CUSTOMERS

CPE/Material Standardization Process and the appointment of Material Standardization Committee detailed in the CEO's Circulars bearing No: 14/2021 and 46/2021 will be amended as follows.

Due to the dynamic and diversified nature of the CPE/Material used for Enterprise and Digital Products, it has been noticed that standardization process is not viable to purchase CPEs/NTUs for urgent and frequently changing business needs of Enterprise customers.

Considering the above, it has been decided to follow the standard purchase and governance process of CAPEX procurement of goods and services within the TOR of Enterprise Investment Management Committee (EIMC) and Enterprise Solutions Techno Commercial Evaluation Team (ES-TCET), when purchasing CPEs/NTUs for Enterprise product requirements, which are subjected to frequent changes.

CPE standardization process given in CEO circulars 14/2021 and 46/2021 will continue to be applicable for the rest of the CPEs.

K A Kiththi Peyera Chief Executive Officer
