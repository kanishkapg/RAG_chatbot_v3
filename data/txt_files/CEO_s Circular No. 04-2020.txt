CEO's Circular No: O4 / 2020

CEO's Office, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 01.

22 January 2020

To All Staff,

POSTS OF

1. SENIOR EXECUTIVE ASSISTANT MANAGER (CUSTOMER RELATIONS) – A6
2. SENIOR EXECUTIVE ASSISTANT MANAGER (IT) – A6
3. SENIOR EXECUTIVE ASSISTANT MANAGER (STORES) – A6

Applications are invited from the permanent, confirmed SLT employees who satisfy the under mentioned requirements to be considered for the above mentioned positions.

A. ELIGIBILITY
Candidates should have completed five (05) years' service as a Senior Assistant Manager (Customer Relations) - A7 / Senior Assistant Manager (IT) A7 / Senior Assistant Manager (Stores) A7 in SLT as at 1st January 2020.

B. SELECTION METHOD
1. Selection will be made through an examination and an interview.
2. Examination will be held in English Medium.
3. Interview will be held only for successful candidates of the examination for the final selection.

The subjects specified for the examination are as follows:
i) SLT Procedures, Practices & Business Affairs (40 minutes)
ii) Subject Knowledge
- Part I - Multiple Choice Questions (45 minutes)
- Part II - Essay Type Questions (1 % hours)

C. PLACEMENT
Selected candidates will be promoted as Senior Executive Assistant Manager (Customer Relations) - A6 / Senior Executive Assistant Manager (IT) – A6 / Senior Executive Assistant Manager (Stores) – A6 Grade and placed in the vacant positions. No transfers will be given until the officer completes four (04) years of service in deployed positions.

D. METHOD OF APPLYING
Eligible candidates should fill in the attached application form and forward to DGM/ HR Operations, 7th Floor, HQ Building, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Lotus Road, Colombo 01 through the relevant General Manager / Deputy General Manager.

E. CLOSING DATE
Applications should be sent within seven (07) working days of issuing this circular. Applications sent after the closing date will not be considered.

K. A. Kiththi Peppa
Chief Executive Officer
