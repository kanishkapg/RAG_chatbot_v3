GCEO’s Circular No: 04 /2021

GCEO’s Office, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 01.

02nd August 2021

To All Staff,

SLT GROUP LEGAL STRUCTURE

Please be informed that the SLT Board has approved the framework for the “SLT Group Legal Panel” to function under the Group Chief Executive Officer to provide necessary Legal expertise to Strategic Legal matters pertaining to Group Legal work for the SLT Group of Companies including Mobitel (Pvt) Ltd, SLT Campus (Pvt) Ltd, SLT Digital Info Services (Pvt) Ltd, SLT Visioncom (Pvt) Ltd, SLT Property Management (Pvt) Ltd, Sri Lanka Telecom Services Ltd, Talentfort (Pvt) Ltd and to standardize Legal services across the SLT Group.

Accordingly Mrs. H D A S A Wijkoon (Chief Legal Officer – SLT PLC) will be appointed as the Group Chief Legal Officer (GCLO) of SLT and to be as the coordinator for the SLT Group Legal Panel with immediate effect.

We wish her all success in her new job role.

Lalith Seneviratne
Group Chief Executive Officer
