GUIDELINES ON RESUMPTION OF OPERATIONS IN SLT PLC

I sincerely thank you for your support, dedication and commitment amidst great personal difficulties to continue SLT's business operations, since the start of Sri Lanka's response to the COVID -19 pandemic.

GoSL has decided to relax the curfew from 28th June 2020 onwards with the objective of restoring normalcy in the civilian life. Accordingly, SLT PLC is also initiated resumption of its activities 'Back to the Normalcy' with effect from 1st July 2020 in par with the national guidelines issued by the Government and Health Authorities.

Following guidelines will be implemented with effect from 1st July 2020.

a. Resumption of duties for all staff as per normal duty time/ standard duty rosters.
b. The employees should report to work as per the instructions of the respective supervisors. Each Chief Officer/ GMM / DGMM are authorized to deploy staff under their purview based on the recommended guidelines to achieve the highest productivity.
c. Additionally, employees shall adhere to all other regulations and guidelines issued by the Government and Health Authorities with respect to the prevention of COVID-19. All employees herewith requested to strictly behave with self-discipline while paying attention to the health and wellbeing of colleagues and society to prevent the spread of the disease.

K A Kithibi Perera
Chief Executive Officer
