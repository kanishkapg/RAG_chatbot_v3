CEO's Circular No. 34 / 2020

CEO's Office, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 01 30th June 2020

To: All Executive Staff,

GUIDELINES ON INCURRING OF EXPENSES

Reference to the Circular number 20/2020 issued in April 2020.

Please note that the discretionary expenses need to be incurred, after seeking prior approval from the Finance division (either from GM Finance, Reporting & Tax – Mr. Indrajith Priyantha or Group CFO).

There will be no restrictions on incurring expenses on urgent repairs and maintenance work or any other work directly related to the Operations.

All Capex expenses including "work in progress" need to be deferred or obtained supplier credit in consultation with the suppliers or obtain a special approval for business critical capex from SIMC by emphasizing the importance and urgency.

Further, this restriction is not applicable for Petty cash expenses (Opex), where the limit is Rs. 20,000 per transaction. This is applicable till the year end.

Thank you for your co-operation.
