CEO’s Circular No: 54/2020

CEO’s Office, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 01.
30th September 2020

To All SLT Staff,

Opportunities to Change the Designation

It is pleased to inform that SLT Board has approved to give a special opportunity to the employees who have acquired the qualification stated against each designation below and fulfill the other criteria to consider them for changing of designation. Accordingly, applications are invited from permanent employees who joined SLT before 31.01.2020.

1. Qualification & Eligibility Criteria Required for Each Position.

TELECOMMUNICATION TECHNICAL OFFICER-A9

a) G.C.E. (O/L) examination with six passes with credit passes in Sinhala/Tamil, English Language and Mathematics in one sitting, and 03 passes at the G.C.E. (A/L) in one sitting. AND
b) A Higher Diploma in Telecommunication/Electronic/Communication /Computer/ Electrical equivalent to National Vocational Qualification 6 (NVQ-6) or SLQF 4 or above from a University/Institute recognized by UGC Sri Lanka or any other government authorities. OR
c) Registration at Engineering Council, Sri Lanka as an “Engineering Diplomate” in relevant field.

TELECOMMUNICATION TECHNICAL OFFICER-A10

a) G.C.E. (O/L) examination with six passes with credit passes in Sinhala/Tamil, English Language and Mathematics in not more than two sittings, and 03 passes at the G.C.E. (A/L) in not more than two sittings. AND
b) A Higher Diploma in Telecommunication/Electronic/Communication /Computer/ Electrical equivalent to National Vocational Qualification 6 (NVQ-6) or SLQF 4 or above from a University/Institute recognized by UGC Sri Lanka or any other government authorities. OR
c) Registration at Engineering Council, Sri Lanka as an “Engineering Diplomate” in relevant field.

MARKETING OFFICER-A9

a) Six subjects in the G.C.E. (O/L) examination in one sitting with four credit passes including Sinhala / Tamil, English Language & Mathematics and G.C.E. (A/L) examination with 03 passes in one sitting. AND
b) A Diploma in Marketing from SLIM/CIM Part II or Advanced Diploma in Marketing from a University/Institute recognized by UGC-Sri Lanka or any other government authorities, equivalent to NVQ-6 or SLQF 4 or above.

IT & NETWORK OFFICER-A9

a) G.C.E. (O/L) Exam with 06 passes in one sitting with 04 credit passes including Sinhala/Tamil, English Language & Mathematics and G.C.E. (A/L) examination with 03 passes in one sitting. AND

AND
b) Passed G.C.E. (A/L).
AND
c) Certificate in Marketing equivalent to NVQ Level 5.

2. Other Criteria/Conditions
   • This will be implemented as a special one-time scheme.
   • Posting and placement will be done on par with organizational policy and operational priorities.
   • Only shortlisted applicants will be contacted by HR.

3. Method of Selection
   Selection will be made through a written examination, computer based practical test/trade test/driving test and only those who achieve required competency at the examination/test will be called to interviews for selection.
   Subjects specified for the examination are as follows:
    (a) Subject Knowledge (2 hours)
    i) Multiple Choice Questions (45 Minutes)
    ii) Essay Type Questions (1 ¼ hours)
    (b) Intelligence Quotient & General Knowledge (1 hour)

4. Method of Applying
   A4-size application prepared as per the specimen attached as annexure 1 should be sent through relevant A5 or above officer reach below address within 7 working days of issuing this circular.
