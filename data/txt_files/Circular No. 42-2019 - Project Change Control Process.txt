ENHANCEMENT OF PROJECT CHANGE CONTROL PROCESS

Change control is an important part of the project management process. Changes are sometimes inevitable, so that it is important to introduce proper change control process for ensuring project success. With the pace of change today, it is almost certain that projects will face the demands for changes during their life. While change may help ensure the project's alignment with business needs, it is important to consider and submit each change after proper assessment of the impact to the project, program, other related projects and business.

With the introduction of Investment Management Framework and migration to Program world, the existing change control process of projects has been enhanced to meet above requirement.
The change control process given below, ensures that each change proposed for an approved project is adequately defined, reviewed and approved before implementation.

However maximum effort should be taken by the project planning [planning group and user departments] and delivery teams [Program Managers, Project sponsors & Project Managers] to ensure that minimal changes to the project during its implementation period for effective and efficient project planning and delivery.

Scope of Change Control Process

The Change Control Process is applicable after gate 2 of Investment Management process for change requirement of:

- Scope (change in business needs)
- Cost
- Time
- Changes to the baseline/committed financial business case
- The solution proposed (changes to the architecture or design)
- Approved implementation process by the Investment Management Committee

The high level change control process is given in the Annex-1 of this circular.

1 of 4

Proposing a Change

This process gives the ability for anyone in the project team (including the customers, users) to suggest a change to the project. The change proposal shall be submitted using the new Change Request [CR] template released. Please refer Intranet>General>Investment Management Framework Portal for the template.

http://insite.slt.com.lk/Investment%20Management%20Framework/SitePages/Home.aspx

Special attention is to be given to provide:

• Details of the cost estimates, forecasted detailed time plan in case of substantial changes in a project.
• Validated revised financial business case, when additional Budget is required. (Initially will apply for strategic projects of more than Rs. 50 Million including CR cost and later as advised by the SIMC)
• Details of all other impacted projects, business activities, business units and etc.

Impact Assessment

Impact assessment shall be carried out by the project manager, program manager and impact assessment team on:

• Quantifiable cost savings and benefits
• Estimated cost of the change
• Impact on timescales
• Extra resources needed
• The solution proposed (Changes to the architecture or design)
• Impact on other projects, program(s), business activities, business units
• New risks and issues

Approval Authority

The relevant IMC shall recommend or approve whether to carry out the change as per the delegated authority as given below.

This process involves a review of the change request by following approving authority, who will consider all the information provided by the project manager, program Manager and impact assessment team.

For the following, approval shall be obtained from respective IMC for:

- Changes to the Baseline/Committed Financial Business case due to the CR. (Initially will apply for Strategic Projects of more than Rs. 50 Million including CR cost and later as advised by SIMC)
- Major changes to the solution proposed (Changes to product, architecture or design)
- Approved implementation process

Implementing a Change

If the change is approved, the relevant secretary of the IMC shall inform all related parties including the Program office. If there is a change to the solution proposed, (Changes to the architecture or design) it shall be well informed to GMMs of Planning Group by the relevant secretary of the IMC.

This circular is effective with immediate effect and will supersede GCEO circular 22/2016.
