URGENT PROCESS REENGINEERING ON MATERIAL MANAGEMENT, ASSETS MANAGEMENT, DOCUMENTATION AND SPACE MANAGEMENT

1. Introduction

In view of building up close relationship with the distance staff I visited almost all the regions and Western Province on 1st, 2nd and 6th April 2019 (Saturday and Sunday) with CEO, COO and couple of Chief Officers, Deputy Chief Officers, GMM, DGMM and other related officers in the field. It made me shocked at most of the places visited and wondered the manner in which they managed the staff, material, space, process and documentation etc.

2. Position briefing

Overall Material Management of SLT found very critical and unattended by the Officers responsible. This area has been badly neglected as a whole. Average annual cost of Rs. 10.0 to 15.0 billion of operational cost is incurred on the items such as CPs, Cables, network equipment, drop wires and other electronic equipment etc.

The series of material management activities that includes planning and ordering materials as well as getting the items to the stores \ field, accounting for any issues and returns and keeping accurate tabs on inventory including used materials is an area in which telecom industry tracks others.

SLT materials management should be a discipline. It has not been consistently looked at or well managed. There is a lot of money at stake, and there is a lot of money being wasted." Hence Reengineering Process is badly needed

3. Field Visits

a. Slave Island Building Complex

Status of the building, space management and office management, SME Sector Management and call Box were heavily unsatisfactorily maintained. It is surprised that how GM, DGM and other responsible officers have played their roll under this environment.

Items were scattered haphazardly and management of material was critical. Articles and employees were badly affected due to poor environmental condition and no pest control in operation creating location risk.

f. Locators / Transit Stores

Island wide locators have been identified as they are at a risk and hence number of locators should be reduced. Total process has to be reengineered creating value addition

Further the existing locators should be immediately attended with introduction of proper inventory systems allocating suitable and responsible staff fixing individual duties

Locaters established should be provided with office facilities and In Charge of locators should arrange to maintain updated records on all the material transactions. They also should think of workforce management and process reengineering for the locators under their purview. Using ERP system itemized monthly report should be generated with values

g. Kotugoda Store

First we should appreciate the services of the members attached to Kotugoda Stores for their achievements in this large store activities. The effort of DGM Stores Management with his staff has prepared Dashboard with decent innovative ideas.

Further precautions should be taken to safeguard the items stored outside the premises providing shelters providing protection. Items which needs protection can also transfer to Peliyagoda stores considering the viability of such arrangement or work program should be submitted for alternative arrangement with the tentative budget.

COO is providing his service for stores management coordination. This should be continued as a team with the involvement of GCSMO, GMM Operations, Project GM, RTOM, OPMC Managers, DITs and other related officials

4. Recommendations

1. Overall island wide inventory management system should be critically evaluated and implemented proper system with follow up procedure continuously as we observed heavy risks on wastages due to negligence and carelessness

2. Immediate action should be taken to reengineer the Stores Management Process through Flow Charts. End to end process should be analyzed and fixed the accountability and responsibility on each and every officer responsible

3. Process should be reengineered providing facilities to register all the issues and receipts of stores items at LOCATORS with quantities and Values. Through invoicing system, arrangement may be made to issue Dummy cheques authorizing GMM, DGMM and Accountants to sign them.

4. Management Dashboard should be updated generating following reports on weekly basis.
a. Total and individual purchases during the period
b. Issues during the period in total and item wise
