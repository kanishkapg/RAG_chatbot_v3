GRIEVANCE HANDLING COMMITTEE

The SLT management decided to establish a new grievance handling committee to the 
expeditious settlement of grievances of employees and ensure the creation and sustenance of 
healthy employer-employee relationship. The committee will have oversight responsibilities, 
duties and authority with respect to the grievances received from employees.

The Grievance Committee shall consider only individual grievances of SLT employees 
and raised individually by the concerned aggrieved employee. The Grievance Committee 
shall not consider any grievance generally applicable or collectively raised by more than one 
employee.

The Grievance Committee will meet at least once a month and least three members of the 
Grievance Committee shall be present in a meeting.

Terms of Reference (ToR)

- The Grievance Committee shall consider all grievances submitted in writing by an 
individual employee on his/her career progression, working conditions or any other 
alleged injustice done to an employee while discharging his duties at the work place.
- The Grievance Committee shall have access to all files confidential or otherwise 
relevant to the individual's grievance.
- The Committee shall study the written request and after looking into the relevant 
details to discuss with those concerned and submit its recommendations and report to 
CEO as expeditiously as possible, but in any case within a month of the date of 
complaint.
- In case of any difficulties, the Grievance Committee shall have discussion with CEO 
before a decision is taken.

- The final settlement of any grievance shall be made within a reasonable period (not exceeding one month) after the recommendations are submitted to the CEO by the Grievance Committee.

The aggrieved member shall submit his/her complaint directly to the Chairman of the Grievance Committee with a copy to his/her supervising officer.
