CEO's Circular No. 51/2020

CEO's Office, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 01.
29th September, 2020

To All Executives,

STRENGTHENING PROJECT CASH FLOW PLANNING

Since SLT invests substantial amount of Capital Expenditure (CAPEX) annually, it is extremely important to monitor and control Capital Expenditure budgets effectively, where accurate cash disbursement plans would play a vital role. In order to strengthen the planning of cash disbursements, below listed details shall be gathered from Project Managers (specimens of the templates are annexed to this circular) enabling Finance Department to decide on the future CAPEX requirements of SLT more accurately.

Cash Disbursement Plan

Project Managers (PMM) are required to prepare CAPEX Disbursement Plan for each project and should submit to CAPEX Administration Section as described below;

- In the case of 'ABP approved Ongoing projects';
  - At the time of ABP planning
    - 'Monthly cash disbursement for the ABP year in concern', 'quarterly for the next year' and 'yearly for the subsequent years' in accordance with the Template(1.1) annexed.

- In the case of 'New projects';
  - At the time of ABP planning
    - 'Quarterly disbursement for the ABP year in concern' and 'yearly for subsequent years' in accordance with the Template (1.2) annexed.

- After obtaining G2 Approval from Investment Management Committees for the ABP approved new projects
    - 'Monthly cash disbursement for the ABP year in concern', 'quarterly for the next year' and 'yearly for the subsequent years' in accordance with the Template(1.1) annexed.

Next Three Month Cash Requirement

Once PMM submit the estimated cash disbursement plans as described above, then PMM should submit the project cash requirement relating to next three (3) months in accordance with the Template(2) annexed. This shall be submitted on or before 25th of every month by email to the CAPEX Administration section, which consists of:
• Exact cash requirement specified for the immediate next month based on the payment confirmed invoices
• Accurate cash requirement specified for two subsequent months coming after the immediate next month based on anticipated invoices received relating to the work nearly completed.

Next three months' cash requirement provided with Template (2) is not supposed to deviate drastically from the cash disbursement plan originally provided with Template (1.1), unless otherwise a strong justification is provided. However, based on the availability of cash, additional cash requirements (as specified in Template-2) may be deferred and notified.

All the CXOs are hereby requested to instruct the project managers under their purview to adhere to this circular with immediate effect to ensure cash flow planning and disbursement.

Accurate cash flow planning is one of the key responsibilities of Project Managers and this will be a Key Performance Indicator (KPI).
