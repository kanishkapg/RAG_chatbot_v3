ESTABLISHING OPMC-HAVELOCK TOWN & OPMC-MARADANA  

In order to align the operational work with RTO-Havelock Town and RTO-Maradana established under CEO's Circular No. 67/2020, OPMC-Havelock Town & OPMC-Maradana will be established with effective from 1st March 2021. Accordingly operational boundaries come under each OPMC will be as follows.  

| OPMC's Name    | LEAs Coming Under the OPMC |
|---|---|
| OPMC-Havelock Town    | HK, KPT, HAV, WEL, NAR    |
| OPMC-Maradana    | SI, CEN, MTK CIIR, MD, WE    |
