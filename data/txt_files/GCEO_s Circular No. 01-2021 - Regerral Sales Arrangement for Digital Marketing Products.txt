Referral Sales Arrangement for Digital Marketing Products and Services Offerings of SLT Digital Info Services (Pvt) Ltd

SLT Digital Info Services (Pvt) Ltd (SLTDS), a fully owned subsidiary of Sri Lanka Telecom PLC (SLT) has diversified its business to provide a wide range of digital services to Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) and Micro Businesses. They presently carry out digital marketing activities and web development (entire product range is given in Annexure 01). SLTDS as a subsidiary, offers these products to the customers on behalf of SLT.

The management has decided to offer SLT staff a valuable opportunity to bring in sales leads to SLTDS and earn a referral fee (additional income). The role of SLT staff will be to approach a potential customer and create awareness. If the customer is interested, that lead is to be passed to SLTDS and SLTDS representative will contact the customer directly for further action. End-to-end customer coordination and after sales service will be handled by the SLTDS team. Therefore no interaction required with the customer to deliver the product or service acquired by the customer. Detailed process with the required forms are as per Annexure 02.

If the lead generated by the SLT staff member is successful, SLTDS will make a referral payment to the SLT staff member (via the relevant paying officer) after receiving payments from the customer.

The referral fee structure is as follows:

| Product    | Referral fee for SLT staff |
|---|---|
|    | Invoice value w/o tax < 100,000    | Invoice value w/o tax 100,001 to 500,000    | Invoice value w/o tax > 500,000    |
| Digital Marketing   | 5% of the total invoice value    | 4% of the total invoice value (Minimum to be paid Rs. 5,000)    | 3% of the total invoice value (Minimum to be paid Rs. 20,000)    |
| Web   | 7% of the total invoice value    | 5% of the total invoice value (Minimum to be paid Rs. 7,000)    |    |

All SLT staff are invited join this program which would benefit you personally and the SLT Group as a whole.

Amexure 02: Referral Sales Process

1. SLT Staff should register with SLTDS, before starting the lead submission (find the attached form - SLT Staff Registration form - v.01)
2. SLT staff may discuss with customer to identify the requirements and inform SLTDS briefly in the space provided (refer Lead generation form - v.01). Eg – Digital marketing , web development, IVR etc..
3. SLTDS will contact the customer within 24Hrs in order to get more details.
4. Thereafter, SLTDS will directly deal with customer in order fulfil the customer requirement.
5. SLTDS will share the project details (Eg – payment plan, time duration etc..) with the relevant SLT staff member, after customer has confirmed.
6. SLTDS will collect the payment directly; however SLT staff can assist to expedite the payment.
7. SLTDS transfer referral commission fee to be paid via SLT paying officer along with salary. This information will be shared with relevant DGM mentioned in the SLT Staff Registration Form, in order to assess the extra contribution to SLT group.
8. SLTDS will send all referral commission details to SLT paying officer by 10th of the following month, in order to make such payment along with salary.
9. Referral commission will be paid based on the collected revenue in the previous month.
