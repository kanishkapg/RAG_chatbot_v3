CEO's Circular No: 35/2020

CEO's Office, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 01.

13 July 2020

To All Executive Staff,

POST OF DGM-COLLECTION & CREDIT CONTROL

Applications are invited from the permanent executive staff of A4 and A5 grades of SLT, who satisfy the under mentioned requirements to be considered for the above post.

1. ELIGIBILITY

1.1 FOR OFFICERS PRESENTLY IN A4 GRADE:

(a) Accounting Profession:
(i) Associate Membership of ICA/CIMA/ACCA with 3 years Accounting / Auditing experience in A4 Grade in SLT
    OR
(ii) Intermediate qualification of ICA/CIMA/ACCA with 5 years Accounting / Auditing experience in A4 Grade in SLT
    OR
(iii) 7 years Accounting / Auditing experience in A4 Grade in SLT

1.2 FOR OFFICERS PRESENTLY IN A5 GRADE:

(a) Accounting Profession:
(i) Associate Membership of ICA/CIMA/ACCA with 3 years Accounting / Auditing experience in A5 Grade in SLT
    OR
(ii) Intermediate qualification of ICA/CIMA/ACCA with 6 years Accounting / Auditing experience in A5 Grade in SLT
    OR
(iii) 8 years Accounting / Auditing experience in A5 Grade in SLT

1.3 Officers in A4 Grade who do not possess required number of experience in A4 Grade under category 1.1 will be eligible under category 1.2 by adding the number of years experience in A5 Grade and A4 Grade together and those officers may also apply.

2. OPPORTUNITY FOR PRESENT DGMs & OTHER OFFICERS IN A3 GRADE DGMs (A3/A4) or any other A3 grade officers in accounting profession who wish to be considered for the above position may also apply.

3. SELECTION METHOD
Selection will be by an interview.

4. PLACEMENT
Selected candidate will be initially appointed as DGM-Collection & Credit Control (Acting). Subsequent to serving in the acting capacity for 06 months' period, if the selected officer is in A4 Grade, he /she will be placed in A3 Grade and if the selected officer is in A5 Grade he / she will be placed in A4 Grade and will be confirmed in the respective position.

5. METHOD OF APPLYING
Eligible candidates should fill in the attached application form and forward to DGM/HR Operations through the relevant Supervising Officer.

6. CLOSING DATE
Applications should be sent within 07 days of issuing this circular.

K. A. Kiththi Perera  
Chief Executive Officer
