CEO’s Circular No: 73 /2020

CEO’s Office, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 01.

31 December 2020

To: All General Managers,

POST OF GENERAL MANAGER – IS & QM

Applications are invited from the present General Managers who satisfy the under mentioned requirements to be considered for the above post.

1. OPPORTUNITY FOR TRANSFER
GMs (A1/A2/A3) who wish to be transferred for the above position shall apply.

2. SELECTION METHOD
Selection will be by an interview.

3. PLACEMENT
Selected General Manager will be transferred as GM - IS & QM.

4. METHOD OF APPLYING
Eligible candidates should fill in the attached application form and forward to GM-HR & IR (Acting) through the relevant Supervising Officer.

5. CLOSING DATE
Applications should be sent within 07 days of issuing this Circular.

K. A. Kijthir Perera
Chief Executive Officer
