AMENDMENT TO THE CEO'S CIRCULAR NO. 29/2021 DATED 17TH MAY 2021

EXTERNAL RECRUITMENT OF SALES OFFICER (A12) TO SLT (OPEN ONLY FOR SLT EMPLOYEES' CHILDREN).

With reference to the CEO's Circular No. 29/2021 on "External Recruitment of Customer Care Officer (A10), Sales Officer (A12) and Technician (A15) to SLT (open only for SLT Employees' Children)" issued on 17th May 2021, the recruitment of Customer Care Officer - A10 will be cancelled and the recruitment qualification of Sales Officer - A12 will be amended as stipulated below.

Accordingly, applications are invited from children of SLT employees who are possessing below stated qualifications and fulfill the other eligibility criteria as at 30th November 2021 to be considered for vacancies stated above.

1. Qualification & Eligibility Criteria for the post Sales Officer-A12.
   a) G.C.E. (O/L) six subjects in not more than two sittings with four credit passes including ordinary passes in Arithmetic/Mathematics and Sinhala or Tamil language/Literature and credit pass in English language/Literature. AND
   b) Passed G.C.E. (A/L) in one sitting AND
   c) Upper Age Limit is 35 Years.

Note: The applications which are already received for the Customer Care Officer - A10 and Sales Officer- A12 under the CEO's Circular No. 29/2021 will be considered for the new selection process of Sales Officer – A12 as per the qualifications stated hereof, and the eligibility date specified in the CEO's Circular No. 29/2021.

The candidates selected at the interview should complete a training course leading to NVQ 5 in marketing at Telecom Training Center (for the training course at TTC, a nominal fee will be charged from these employees) or equivalent external qualification obtained from a recognized institution during their probationary period before the confirmation in service.

2. Criteria and other conditions applicable to employees to make eligible their children to the scheme.

a) SLT Permanent employees who are currently working in SLT and having more than 10 years of confirmed and satisfactory service in SLT as at 30th November 2021.

b) SLT Permanent retired employees who were working in SLT during last 10 years as at 30th November 2021 and having completed more than 10 years of confirmed and satisfactory service in SLT at the date of retirement.

c) The benefit will only be given to one child of SLT employee. Employees whose child/children were recruited under the previous "employees' children scheme" are not eligible to the scheme.

d) When both husband & wife (parents of applicant) are employees/retired employee(s) in SLT, only one child is eligible to the scheme.

3. Method of Selection.
Selection will be made through an examination. Only those who achieve required competency at the examination will be called for an interview for final selection. Placement will be done on par with organization's operational priorities.

4. Method of Applying
Application should be sent via online. Visit www.sltmobitel.lk (careers page) and complete the online application on your own, submit with curriculum vitae including a recent photograph together with scanned copies of specimen application form attached herewith as annexure 1, birth, educational and other relevant certificates within 7 working days of issuing this circular.

Applications without supporting documents will not be entertained and applications sent by post will not be accepted.

Application link is available at the SLT website: www.sltmobitel.lk (visit careers page). Attachments file size should not exceed 15 MB.

Sri Lanka Telecom PLC is an equal opportunity employer and any type of canvassing will be a disqualification. Only shortlisted applicants will be contacted by HR.

K A Kiththi Perera
Chief Executive Officer
