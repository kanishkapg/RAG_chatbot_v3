CEO’s Circular No: 65/2019

CEO’s Office, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 01.
29th November 2019

To All SLT Staff,

Internal Recruitment of Senior Executive Assistant Manager (A6)

Applications are invited from the permanent, confirmed SLT employees who possess the following qualifications for the above post.

1. Eligibility

a) Five years of permanent confirmed and satisfactory service in SLT as at 30th November 2019. AND
b) Three-year degree in Management (in a business related specific field)/Business Administration from a state university of Sri Lanka. OR
c) Three-year general degree (in any field) from a state university of Sri Lanka or equivalent, which is equivalent to NVQ 7 or SLQF 5 or above With Post Graduate Diploma in Management/Business Administration etc. or above (in a business related specific field) from a state university of Sri Lanka. AND
d) Should have obtained minimum Performance Rating of Level 3 or above in the immediately preceding 03 years at the Annual Performance Review.

2. Method of Selection

Selection will be made through a written examination (English Medium) and interviews. Subjects specified for the examination are as follows:

(a) Subject Knowledge (2 hrs)
    i) Multiple Choice Questions (45 Minutes)
    ii) Essay Type Questions (1 ¼ hrs)
(b) Intelligence Quotient & General Knowledge (1 hrs)

The date and the time table of the examination will be notified in due course.

3. Method of Applying

Application should be prepared on an A4-size paper as per the specimen attached herewith, and should be sent through relevant GM/DGM to reach the DGM-HR Strategies & Policy, Human Resources Division, 7th Floor, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 01 within 7 working days of issuing this circular.

K A Kithini Perera
Chief Executive Officer
