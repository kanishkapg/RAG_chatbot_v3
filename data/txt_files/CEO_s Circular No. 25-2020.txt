CEO's Circular No: 25/2020

CEO's Office, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 01.

2nd June 2020

To All Executives,

PLACEMENTS OF NEWLY RECRUITED STAFF FROM HCS TO SLT

All the new staff members, who were recruited from HCS to SLT with appointment letter dated 1st June 2020 are advised to report for duties to their current work stations for the time being, until deployed in respective stations in due course.

Further, those employees have to continue their previously assigned job functions until their placements are informed. Therefore, please do the necessary arrangements accordingly.

K A Kiththi Peera Chief Executive Officer
