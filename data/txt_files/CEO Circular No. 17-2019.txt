STRENGTHENING PROGRAM MANAGEMENT

Successful implementation of Long Range Strategic Plan (LRSP) and Annual Business Plan (ABP) heavily depends on the management of Projects, Programs and Investment Governance by adopting Best Management Practices.

Under ABP 2019, SLT has defined 11 Revenue Generating Programs & 13 Enabler Programs with all the Projects and initiatives mapped to those. Also, Program Management organizations has been redefined along with recent organizational changes and in par with Profit Centre concept.

• Program Director [Prog Dir.] at chief officer level has been appointed for each program, who will undertake the leading responsibility for the program delivery and the realization of the targeted benefits of the program.
• A Program Manager/Delivery has been appointed in the General Manager level for each program, who will be responsible for the delivery of targeted capabilities/capacities provided by various projects coming under the program.
• A Program Manager/Benefit Realization has been appointed in the General Manager level for each revenue generating program and will take a principal responsibility for realizing targeted benefits.

At Project level, Project sponsor will be accountable for the success of the project. Project Manager will be responsible to deliver the project as per the approval given by the relevant Investment Management Committee [IMC] for the Project plan, cost, scope and the business case. In most of the Projects, Program Director and Project Sponsor will be the same individual.

Under the implementation of ABP 2019, each Program will be allocated with a specified amount of CAPEX and the relevant Program Director is responsible for the optimum utilization of allocated financial resources in order to maximise the realization of targeted benefits. The Program Director together with the Program Manager can decide on the original distribution (and subsequent re-distribution) of the above allocated program CAPEX among the projects under their program, subject to the approval of the relevant Investment Management Committee (IMC).
