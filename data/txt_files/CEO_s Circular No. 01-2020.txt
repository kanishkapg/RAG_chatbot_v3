DECLARATION OF THE YEAR OF CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE – 2020

In today's digital world being the pioneering Digital Service Provider (DSP) in communication, SLT is in the process of reinventing the 'Customer Experience', to keep up with the new kind of "always-connected" customer. Therefore, reimagining 'Customer Experience' to meet the changing needs of the customer in this digital era, is a primary driver for today's and future of SLT's business. We strongly believe that customer experience, the sum total of all the interactions a customer has with SLT during his/her journey with us is the critical factor in building and maintaining the competitive advantage in this hyper - competitive, hyper-connected global market.

Therefore, to become the Customer Experience Leader in the market, SLT stands a chance to declare Year 2020 as the Year of 'Customer Experience'.

The prime objective of this exercise is to make the digital life style of customers more convenient and beneficial to their expected level. Acknowledging the fact that our customers are the masters of our destiny and the drivers of SLT's growth, we must provide customers with consistent, compelling experiences - before, during, and after their journey with us across all channels seamlessly from every touch point.

Digitalization has made the customers "always connected" with us. Digital growth of SLT is coming at the expense of customer experience, with a new approach offers that fits the digital demands of the customers. Better products, and unmatched service that fulfill and assure the digital demands of customers paves the way to build the trust and loyalty towards the organization. This unique customer experience that fits the today's expectations of customers make SLT brand to stand differently from the other market players in the areas of, responsibility, accountability, customer driven, honesty and also positive employee engagement.

As customer experience strategy is an ongoing process, we need to adopt a continuous improvement approach as a team effort of all the members of SLT who are more fully, in direct contact with the customers and also who support the same best experience through technology, systems and processes. We need to adopt a continuous improvement work ideology to meet changes in customer behavior, their needs and their expectations that drive by the digital world.

More fully, customer experience is also a market opportunity that creates an environment to uplift SLT business by the customers as SLT products and service promoters. To achieve this target of becoming the leader in customer experience let us work together while exploiting the biggest differentiator in the industry ultimately to propel the SLT brand to the prime position it deserves.

K A Kiththj-Pérera
Chief Executive Officer
