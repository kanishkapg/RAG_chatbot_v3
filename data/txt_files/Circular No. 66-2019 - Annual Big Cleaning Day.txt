CEO’s Circular No. 66/2019

CEO’s Office, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 01.

10 December 2019

To: All Employees,

DECLARATION OF SLT ANNUAL BIG CLEANING DAY – 2019 “Work place - New look for New Year”

I hereby declare 19th of December 2019 as SLT Annual Big cleaning day to carryout 5S activities, which will create a new look at your work place for the New Year.

On this day all employees should engage in 5S activities and keep all offices clean and clutter free.

This will allow all employees to work in a pleasant work atmosphere and improve work quality and productivity.

Furthermore, it is required to take suitable measures to minimize any inconvenience to customers on this big cleaning day.

Further information on this day activities will be informed via QA-INFO mail by Quality Management Section.

I expect the active participation of all employees on this day and wish all the success for SLT Annual Big Cleaning Day Programme.

K A Kiththi Pepera Chief Executive Officer
