To : All Employees,

Implementation of Annual Business Plan 2019

In order to facilitate customer centric/market oriented business, new RTO and OPMC areas have been established in the following rapidly developing and urbanizing cities. Regional Enterprise business is also strengthened in WPC Province by splitting the area into two to further increase the SME market share. This transformation in the regional structure is mainly focused on increasing the SLT's customer reach and to improve market penetration in an accelerated pace.

| RTO    | Relevant DGM    | Relevant GM    |
|---|---|---|
| Maharagama    | Western Province South West   | Western Province Central    |
| Piliyandala    | Western Province South West   | Western Province Central    |
| Bandaragama    | Western Province South   | Western Province South    |
| Horowpathana    | North Central Province    | North Central & North Western Province    |

Details of the area demarcations are given under Annexure-1. These new RTO and OPMC area demarcations will be effective from 01st of July, 2019.

P.G. Kumarasinghe Sirisena
Group Chairman

CC : CHRO - Please appoint relevant 4 RTOMs & relevant OPMC Managers as above & establish cost centers and amend the Organization Structure.
CIO - Configure OSS, BSS, CRM, ERP and other relevant IT systems.
DGM/BPR - For information and necessary changes.
