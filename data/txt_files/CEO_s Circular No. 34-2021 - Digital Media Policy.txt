DIGITAL MEDIA POLICY

The Digital Media Policy (Document No: HRIR/DMP/001) sets out the compliance, guidelines and best practices to be followed by Employees of Sri Lanka Telecom PLC when using Digital Media. This Policy shall ensure that Employees engage and/or conduct themselves in a responsible manner on online platforms and to safeguard both Employees and Sri Lanka Telecom PLC while minimizing social, legal and criminal consequences of wrong Digital Media Usage.

Violation of this Policy will be considered to be in violation of Sri Lanka Telecom Code of Conduct and Disciplinary Procedure and will be subject to appropriate disciplinary actions by the disciplinary authority of Sri Lanka Telecom PLC.

The Digital Media Policy (Document No: HRIR/DMP/001) is available at SLT Intranet [General>SLT Common Document Portal>Policies>Human Resources (CPO)>Digital Media Policy]
