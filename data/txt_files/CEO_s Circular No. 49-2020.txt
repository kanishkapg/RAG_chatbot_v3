GUIDELINE FOR PAYMENT TERMS FOR CONTRACTS WITH A PROMISSORY NOTE ON DEFERRED PAYMENT FACILITY.

As per the regulations applicable for Negotiable Instruments, the structure of the Promissory Note requires to be unconditional, if the same is to be considered as a Negotiable Instrument.

In the event of the Promissory Note is issued by SLT, as payment guarantee under a Deferred Payment Contracts, SLT status would be as follows:
1. SLT becomes unconditionally liable for the amount stated under the Promissory Note irrespective of any lacuna, or defect under the Principal contract.
2. The holder becomes entitled to enforce the same against SLT, under a simple process.
3. Once the Promissory Note is issued by SLT, same will be irrevocable.

Taking into account the obligations imposed on SLT due to issuance of a Promissory Note under Principal Contract, with deferred payment facility, the following guidelines are issued for the consideration of the relevant Commercial Evaluation and Project Teams :
a) The payment terms under the Principal Contract to be structured, for the payments to become due subsequent to completion of delivery/ services/ works.
b) If payments are triggered prior to the completion of delivery/ Services/ works under the Principal Contract, in such event adequate security to be obtained for the payments made.
