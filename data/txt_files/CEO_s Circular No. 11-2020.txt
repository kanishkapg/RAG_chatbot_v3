INTRODUCING FLEXIBLE DUTY-OFF TIME FOR NON-EXECUTIVE STAFF

SLT Management is pleased to inform that flexible "Duty-OFF time" has been introduced for non-executive staff working at technical grades, clerical and allied grades and customer care assistant grades, who are presently having duty time of 8:30–17:00Hrs with effect from 01st March 2020 as per following guidelines;

a) If an employee reports to work before the assigned IN time (i.e. 8:30Hrs), he or she will be allowed to leave the office 15 minutes prior to the assigned OUT time (i.e. Assigned Out time is 17:00Hrs and the employee will be allowed to leave the office at 16:45Hrs) without disturbing assigned and operational work.

b) If the employee is requesting to perform overtime, the overtime calculation will be starting from 17:00Hrs onwards.

c) Above flexible "Duty-OFF time" is not applicable for following scenarios;

i. Employee is obtained prior approval to perform pre-OT
ii. Employee is assigned to shift operations

d) Duty time of above employees will remain as 8:30 – 17:00Hrs for all other administrative purposes.

The heads of respective functional units are required to ensure that work is not disturbed and customer services are maintained after the implementation of the above.
