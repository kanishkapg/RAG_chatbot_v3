ANNUAL PERFORMANCE APPRAISAL-2019

Please be informed that the annual performance appraisals for 2019 can be started with immediate effect and the effective duration for the evaluation is period from 01st January 2019 to 31st December 2019. Managing staff performance is a significant supervisory responsibility, because it directly enables staff members to perform as effectively as possible to support departmental and company performance. Please note that all supervising officers are responsible on ensuring completion of effective performance appraisal for all their subordinates and also need to complete their subordinates' appraisal before participating his/her own appraisal interview.

APPRAISAL METHOD
Appraisal of all A10 and above staff (A1 to A10) should be done through ERP system. Appraisal of A11 and below (A11 to A16) staff should be done using manual form.

APPRAISAL PROCESS
Appraisal process consists of 3 steps, namely self-appraisal (appraisee), supervisor appraisal (appraiser) and reviewer appraisal. Completed appraisal should be reached to DGM-HR Strategies & Policy through relevant protocol with the approval of respective Chief Officer on or before the date specified under time plan.

APPRAISAL INTERVIEW
It is compulsory to conduct a year-end performance appraisal interview between appraisee, appraiser and reviewer which will enable them to communicate first-hand information which will form part of their overall assessment. This interview should be conducted for the duration of at least 15 minutes. During the interview all the parties (Appraisee, Appraiser, and Reviewer) may share their views on levels of targets achievements, competencies, gaps and areas to be improved during the next year. It is compulsory to conduct performance appraisal interview and communicate the relevant appraisal rating to the employee with feedback.

APPRAISAL GUIDELINES
Detail information including appraisal forms and guidelines to supervisors and other staff is available intranet. Accordingly, ERP user guide for performance appraisal, performance management procedure document and manual performance appraisal forms applicable for A11 and below staff can be downloaded from the intranet. Please read complete performance management procedure document and ERP user guide prior to commencement of the appraisal.

PERFORMANCE BASED TRAINING
To achieve the ultimate objective of developing employees' performance, necessary training is very important. Right training guides the employees to improve their competencies and provide means of achieving the desirable level in performance.

Supervising officers can recommend training for their subordinates via ERP. Training programs which are not scheduled in the ERP can be requested by using separate training application form available in the intranet. Please refer performance appraisal ERP user manual for more information.

APPRAISAL TIMETARGET
I. Completion of self-appraisal before 15th January 2020.
II. Completion of appraiser-appraisal before 20th January 2020.
III. Completion of reviewer-appraisal on or before 31st January 2020.
IV. HR will submit an appraisal summary report generated from ERP to each GM/DGM for their approval on or before 10th February 2020.
V. Each GM/DGM should submit approved final performance ratings to DGM-HR Strategies & Policy through relevant protocol with the approval of respective Chief Officer on or before 28th February 2020.

Please note that performance ratings approved by respective Chief Officer would be considered as final ratings.
