ESTABLISHMENT OF QUALITY MANAGEMENT FORUM (QMF)

SLT Management has decided to strengthen Quality Management activities in the company by establishing a Quality Management Forum which will be responsible for the success of achieving following key objectives of SLT.

Key Objectives;

Constant improvement of;
- Service quality
- Material quality
- Process quality and efficiency
- Quality of SLT networks and systems
- Skills and competencies of employees
- Operational etiquettes and employee disciplines
- Effective use of tools and equipment and
- Employee behaviors and quality culture

Quality Management Forum (QMF) shall be chaired by CCDO with QM and BCM teams and duly supported by co-chairs of CSRO 1 & 2, CNO and CIO. All the CXOs should nominate their representations with proper empowerment to the member who represent the QM forum with decision making powers.

Other Members of QMF by designation are GM/IS & QM, GM/OS, GM/R & D, DGM/QM, DGM/OR, DGM/CAE, DGM/WPCS and DGM/WPCE.

All employees should support the QM Forum suggestions and new action plans to drive SLT towards Total Quality Management concept and ensure continuous improvements in Quality Assurance in the company aligning with Quality Strategy and Framework annexed herewith.

It is intended to increase awareness in quality with special emphasis on team work within the organization to transform all SLT employees towards quality focused culture, which will enhance customer and employee satisfaction.

K A Kiththi Pefera
Chief Executive Officer
