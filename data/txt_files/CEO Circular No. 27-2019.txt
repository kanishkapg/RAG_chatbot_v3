STRENGTHENING ENERGY MANAGEMENT ACTIVITIES IN SLT

To meet the current challenges in competition, we must seek all opportunities to manage our costs efficiently to enhance profitability levels. While we are examining all areas of company expenditure for such opportunities, energy management has been identified as a key area to focus.

Systematic management of energy, will not only reduce our operational expenditure, but also positively contribute to conserve our environment from greenhouse gas emissions. This further leads to SLT as a responsible corporate citizen, which will favorably contribute to manage the increasing energy demand.

At present SLT spends huge amount of money for energy consumption in the form of Electricity, Water, and Fuel etc. Hence SLT management has decided to strengthen the energy management activities broadening its policy and implementation of it with new strategies.

It is a key responsibility and a moral obligation of each and every employee to participate actively to conserve energy and use it efficiently.

All employees are kindly requested to take up this challenge to assist us in reducing our energy usage through careful & regular attention to your daily energy use, which will substantially contribute to our company as well as to our Ecosystem.

Accordingly, SLT management has established

(1) Energy Management Forum (EMF) co-chaired by CNO & CinCO comprising a cross functional team, who will have the task of further analyzing our energy use & identifying specific initiatives to reduction in energy consumption and

(2) Energy Ambassadors are appointed to drive, implement and sustain energy management activities.

Members of Energy Management Forum & Energy Ambassadors are attached in annexes (1) and (2)

Role of Energy Ambassadors

• To oversee and monitor all energy consuming activities in monthly basis
• Monitoring, taking measurements, analysis, and evaluation for energy performance specially for following cost drivers
o Electricity (Commercial, Generator & PV Solar separately)
o Water
o Generator fuel
o Vehicle fuel

• To provide Energy Management progress reports to EMF
• Carrying out energy audits twice a year with the support of Energy Management Forum
• Identify & implement energy saving opportunities
• Replacement of obsolete and old equipment those are energy inefficient and make arrangements to replace those with the support of Head Office
• Taking space management initiatives for energy saving
• Make the employees aware on energy management activities
• Make employee self-motivated for efficient use of energy
• Setting targets for regional units’ sections to reducing energy consumption 1% through behavioral change strategies
• Achieving KPIs of energy management assigned by EMF
• Ensure that internationally and nationally accepted safety guidelines and other relevant standards are followed in all activities where energy is involved

Below are some general guidelines to follow, though this is not an exhaustive list and SLT management you to take additional measures to improve cost efficiency through appropriate energy management activities. All employees are hereby made responsible for energy management in their respective areas to minimize operational cost and reap other benefits.

• Switch off all electrical items before leaving the office
• Examine and take corrective action in all air conditioned locations, particularly equipment rooms for cooled air leakages to the external environment
• Ensure maximum use of natural light and switch off all or part of the electrical lights in the room, in particular switch off lights when leaving the office even for a short period
• Switch off all non-essential lights and keep only essential lights for security and safety

- Set the temperature of A/Cs to 26°C or above for all comfort A/Cs (It is possible to reduce energy consumption of A/C by 4% if the temperature is increased by 1°C). For equipment rooms, do not set below the manufacturer’s recommended temperature

It has been found that in some equipment room’s temperature is set to 18°C whereas the Manufacturer’s recommendation is 23°C or higher. (Refer CNO’s circular 01/2016-Minimizing Cost of Air-conditioning in SLT)

- Use fans wherever possible instead of A/Cs
- Switch off all computers, printers, photo copying machines and other office equipment, when not in use (standby mode should be avoided as much as possible)
- Assess the possible replacement of electrical items having low energy efficiency with an alternative item having high energy efficiency (subject to reasonable justification)
- Critically review all journeys made using SLT provided vehicles and take corrective action for misuse and other cost inefficiencies
- Ensure use of SLT vehicles as per the vehicle policy
- Review fuel consumption of all vehicles, generators, etc., and take corrective actions to mitigate waste/pilferage. Report all cases where cost efficiency is found to below an acceptable level
- Ensure saving of water (Refer CLO’s circular 01/2017-Saving of water).
- Critically review each and every location/activity for areas of energy consumption that can be reduced or minimized within or outside of your control
- Start building an energy conscious culture

In addition to the above, Energy Management Forum will issue guidelines from time to time as and when required and will provide training/awareness sessions for employees to enhance their knowledge in Energy Management.

SLT management encourages all employees to contribute their ideas, comments to the members of EMF through group email emf@slt.com.lk.
