GUIDELINES FOR RESUMPTION OF OPERATIONS IN SLT HEADQUARTERS
(APPLICABLE FOR WEEK 01 - FROM 11TH TO 17TH MAY 2020)

I sincerely thank you for your support, dedication and commitment amidst great personal
difficulties to continue SLT's business operations, since the start of Sri Lanka's response to the
COVID -19 pandemic.

The GoSL has decided on resumption of government and private sector operations starting from
11th May 2020 and has issued guidelines for the process. Accordingly, Sri Lanka Telecom, as a
responsible corporate entity, will resume its operations in a restrained manner from 11thMay
2020 onwards and will gradually restore normalcy in due course.

All employees must strictly adhere to the following guidelines for resuming activities, while
prioritizing the Health and safety measures of our employees.

1. Working time starts at 10:00 a.m.

2. All DGM and above Officers should attend to work at their respective workstations with
effect from 11th May 2020.

3. Each Chief Officer must nominate employees who shall attend to work/offices within their
group on particular days. The maximum number of employees attending office per
day shall not exceed 30% of the total number of employees in the group. Chief Officers are
authorized to encourage their staff to continue work from home where as possible and
achieve the highest productivity.

4. On days which they are not nominated to attend office, employees shall continue to 'Work
from Home'.

5. All employees who attend to work, shall have their body temperature measured before entering the office premises. All employees shall wear face masks and continue wearing them throughout at work and even during traveling to/from work.

6. Facilities have been provided at the entrance of the office premises for washing hands with soap or sanitizers. All employees must wash their hands using soap or disinfectant before entering to the office premises.

7. Marking of attendance with Finger Scan Machines are required. However, all finger scan machines have already been reconfigured to mark attendance by tapping SLT ID card without scanning the finger, in order to avoid touching the machine.

8. Use stairs in all possible occasions and an elevator is permitted only for 04 persons at a time.

9. Minimize gatherings and always maintain the recommended social distance of more than one meter and confine only on-line meetings in instances, where distance maintenance cannot apply.

10. Chief Officers should vigilantly monitor employees' movements, their body temperature and other activities. Limit employee movements within the premises, unless it is essentially required.

11. Be mindful in using common facilities such as Lunch room, Canteen, etc. to minimize the threat for yourself as well as others and leave such places immediately after using them.

12. Employees with symptoms similar to COVID-19 will not be permitted to enter SLT premises. Such suspicious incidents must be immediately reported to the respective line managers and actions must be taken to send them to the medical center or to an isolation room until further medical care is facilitated.

13. In addition to the above, employees must respect and follow all other regulations and guidelines issued by the Government and Health Authorities with respect to the prevention of COVID-19. All employees are kindly requested to behave with self-discipline paying attention to the health and wellbeing of office colleagues and society to prevent the spread of the disease.

14. "Operational Guidelines on Preparedness and Response Outbreak for setting at SLT" is attached for further details. (Please refer Attachment 1).

15. Operational Procedures for regional staff have been issued by CSRO1and CSRO2.

16. A vigilant committee has been appointed with following members to monitor the proper execution of these guidelines at SLT HQ.
