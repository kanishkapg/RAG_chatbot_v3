MID-YEAR PERFORMANCE REVIEW-2019

As you are aware, the mid-year performance review is an important part of the overall employee performance management process. Productive mid-year review discussion is a fundamental way in which managers can support a culture of on-going feedback, further clarify performance expectations going forward, give priorities and staff recognition and also is helpful to achieve better business results.

Mid-year Performance review for 2019 will be effective for the period from 01st January 2019 to 30th June 2019 and applicable only for the A10 and above staff (A1 to A10). Accordingly, all A10 and above staff are required to review the progress on all the targets as at 30th June 2019 and update the same in the ERP system by each individual. Then the same should be discussed and agreed upon with the relevant supervisory officers. At the discussion the accomplishments should be summarized and later supervisor can make any further changes in the ERP, if required.

All DGM or above officers are requested to ensure the completion of the mid-year review of all staff under his/her purview as per the below stated timeline. HR will monitor the outcome through the ERP, report progress.

Time plan
Step1: Employee should update individual progress in the ERP, on or before 10thJuly 2019.

Step2: Discussion between supervisor and the subordinator on any required further changes of the targets, on or before 20th July 2019.

Step3: Update final achievement of mid-year targets by supervising officer, on or before 31st July 2019.

Annexure 1 describes the steps on how to update in ERP.
