To all Executives,

GUIDELINES ON INCURRING OF EXPENSES

Please note that the discretionary expenses need to be incurred, after seeking prior approvals
obtained from the Finance division (either from GM Finance, Reporting & Tax – Mr. Indrajith
Priyantha or Group CFO). Any expenses which are incurred during this Covid 19 epidemic crisis
on safety measures, transport, meals, etc., in order to provide uninterrupted services to our
customers can be continued by obtaining necessary approvals from the relevant Chief Officer.

There will be no restrictions on incurring expenses on urgent repairs and maintenance work or any
other work directly related to the Operations.

All Capex expenses to be incurred including "work in progress" need to be differed or obtained
supplier credit in consultation with the suppliers and finance or obtain a special approval for
business critical capex from SIMC by emphasizing the importance and urgency.

Further, this restriction is not applicable for Petty cash expenses (Opex), where the limit is Rs.
20,000 per transaction.

This constraint will be in place during the next 3 months until June' 2020 and will be relaxed once
the situation improves.

Thank you for your co-operation.

K A Kiththi Perera
Chief Executive Officer
