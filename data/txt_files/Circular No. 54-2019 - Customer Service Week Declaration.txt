CEO's Office, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 01.

25 September 2019

To All Staff,

DECLARATION OF CUSTOMER SERVICE WEEK 2019 AT SLT

The International Customer Service Association celebrates "International Customer Service Week" every year during the first week of October. Accordingly, they have declared this year's "Customer Service Week" from 07th to 11th October 2019.

In line with the International Customer Service Week, SLT management has decided to celebrate the SLT Customer Service Week (CSW) with the main objective of thanking all our employees for their unstinted dedication towards delivering a superior customer experience and our valued customers for being loyal to SLT.

In order to achieve the above objective "SLT Customer Service Week" was planned with the theme "PRIDE OF SLT". Many activities are scheduled to be held during this week and the inaugural ceremony will commence on the 07th October 2019 at BMICH.

SLT management is expecting your fullest commitment and contribution to ensure that the SLT Customer Service Week enhances even further the commitment Team SLT has undertaken towards extending a better customer experience.

Let us get together and take every effort to ensure that this series of activities will be a success.

K.A. Kihtibi Pérera
Chief Executive Officer
