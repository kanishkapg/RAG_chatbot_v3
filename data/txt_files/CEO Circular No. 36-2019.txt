CEO's Office, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 01.

To: All Staff

SLT “SUWATHA” ANNUAL MEDICAL TESTS SCHEME – 2019

Further to CEO’s Circular No. 35/2019, the SLT “Suwatha” Annual Medical Tests Scheme will be applicable to the age group of 30 – 40 years (Male/Female) employees as usual.

Accordingly, for the above age group of 30 – 40 years the applicable medical tests, list of hospitals, commencing date and terms & conditions will be notified in due course.

K A Kiththi Perera
