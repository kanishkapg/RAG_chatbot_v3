To All Staff,

SUBMISSION OF CONCERNS RELATED TO CAREER PROGRESSION

As a part of service appreciation program, the management has decided to request all employees to forward their concerns related to career progression for further consideration. Above shall be communicated by using the attached form to DGM/HR Operations on or before 30th November 2019.

The management will make every effort to ensure that employee will have a clear career progression which is mutually benefited to the employees as well as to the organization.

Accordingly, arrangement will be made to address employee concerns on promotions in amicable manner.

K. A. Kiththi Perera Chief Executive Officer
