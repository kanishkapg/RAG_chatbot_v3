ADOPTION OF AN ELECTRONIC PROCESS FOR CONTRACTS

Taking into account the health precautions, the convenience and other benefits such as speedy application, cost effectiveness and security, it is decided to transform the current contract process handled manually, in to an electronic process, with effect from 15th October 2020.

Accordingly under the newly implemented electronic process for handling the Contracts, Five main types of transactions are identified for which Process Owners and Nominated Officers are appointed as enumerated under the Table 01 below.

The Scope of the Nominated Officers (on behalf of the Process Owners).

1. Initiation
   The Nominated Officers under each Process Owner are required to initiate the relevant Contracts/Agreements on line.

2. Obtaining of the signatures, subject to finalization of the Contract
   (i) Signatures of the Designated Officers in SLT are to be obtained in accordance with the Contract Signing Authority as already delegated under the CEO Circular bearing No.39 of 2020 (or any amendment thereto) and as per the current SLT process for the authorized signatories for SLT.
   (ii) The signatories of the Customer/ Supplier/ Other party, requires to be defined at the time of initiating the Contract and the process.

3. Storage
   The Nominated Officers are required to retain electronic copies of the finalized Contract/Agreement (with all attachments) and the Certificate of Completion along with the Legal Certification Form.

The above online Contract Process shall not preclude or invalidate the initiation/ processing/ execution/ storage of manual Contracts.
