CEO’s Circular No. 05/2019

CEO’s Office
Sri Lanka Telecom PLC
Colombo 01
14th January 2019

To: All Chief Officers,

FOREIGN TRAVEL APPROVALS

This is to draw your kind attention that all the employees who intend to travel overseas under the following circumstances which is fully or partially funded by SLT should obtain the FTSC approval prior to all other necessary preparations;

1. Official foreign visits, training and visits related to sponsorships offered to SLT
2. Visits related to welfare and industrial relations, such as workshop, seminar or conferences.
3. Attending any other foreign visits which are fully or partially funded by SLT.

All respective Chief Officers are hereby requested to guide your subordinates to adhere to the above instructions with immediate effect.

K A Kitbuki Perera
Chief Executive Officer
