NURSING INTERVENTS FOR NURSING MOTHERS

SLT Management is pleasured to inform that the implementation of the new amendment of SHOP AND OFFICE EMPLOYEES (REGULATION OF EMPLOYMENT AND REMUNERATION) (AMENDMENT) ACT, No. 14 OF 2018 [Provision of nursing intervals for nursing mothers] with immediate effect as follows;

A female employee who is nursing a child under one year of age shall be allowed for two nursing intervals, each interval being not less than one (01) hour until she is nursing a child reached the age of one year. The two nursing intervals stated above shall be in addition to the normal intervals allowed for such employee.

The employees, who wish to get the above concession, should forward attached form (annexure A) through supervisor to the DGM / HR Operations.

An employee who has utilized both maternity leave (84 days) and child care leave (1 year), and reported back to work after the child has reached the age of 1 year shall not be entitled for two nursing intervals stipulated by law.

The management of SLT PLC intend that you will take the full benefit of this opportunity while contributing to the betterment of the organization.

K A Kiththi Pepera
Chief Executive Officer
