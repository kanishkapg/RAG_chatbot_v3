Chairman's Circular No:  11 /2019
Chairman's Office, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 01.

IG December 2019

To All SLT Group's Staff

Appointment of Group Chief Internal Auditor (GCIA)

I am pleased to inform that Mr. Lalith Waragoda has been appointed as Group Chief Internal Auditor (GCIA) of SLT group of companies with effect from 01st November 2019. Mr. Waragoda is a fellow member of Institute of Chartered Accountants of Sri Lanka (ICASL) obtained MBA from PIM and has more than 30 years experience in the field of Accounting. On behalf of SLT group of companies and the senior management team I would like to welcome him and we shall extend our assistance to settle down comfortably within the group.

P G Kumarasinghe Sirisena
Chairman-Sri Lanka Telecom PLC
