To All CXO, DCXO & GMM,

POST OF CHIEF PEOPLE OFFICER
Applications are invited from officers who satisfy the under mentioned requirements to be considered for the above post.

1. CXOs, DCXOs and GMM in A1 Grade
CXOs, DCXOs, GMM in A1 grade and in Engineering, Accounting and Legal professions who wish to be considered for the above position may apply.

2. DCXOs and GMM in A2 Grade

(a) Engineering Profession
Corporate Membership of IESL/IEE(UK)/I MechE(UK)/Institution of Civil Engineers (UK) with 5 years' experience in A2 Grade in SLT.

(b) Accounting Profession
Associate Membership of ICA/CIMA/ACCA with 5 years' experience in A2 Grade in SLT

(c) Legal Profession
An Attorney-at-law and Notary Public with 5 years' experience in A2 Grade in SLT

An MBA in Business Management/HRM from a recognized university with experience in people management will be an added qualification.

3. Selection Method
Selection will be by an Interview

4. Placement
After fulfilling the selection criteria of Executive Staff Recruitment and Promotion Scheme (SRPS) and successful completion of trial period, if the selected officer is in A2 Grade he/she will be placed in A1 Grade.

5. Method of Appling
Eligible candidates should fill in the attached application form and forward to Chief Executive Officer through relevant supervising officer.

Closing Date
Applications should be sent within 07 days of issuing this circular.

K A Kiththi Perera
Chief Executive Officer
