GRIEVANCE HANDLING COMMITTEE FOR NEWLY RECRUITED STAFF FROM SLT HCS TO SLT PLC.

In order to handle grievances of newly recruited staff from SLT HCS to SLT PLC, SLT Management has decided to establish a Grievance Handling Committee with effective from 1st June 2020.

The following committee will handle grievances on the change of designation/grade by matching their qualifications as per the advertisement published on 05th May 2020 and will consider only individual grievances raised by concerned staff with relevant document proofs.
