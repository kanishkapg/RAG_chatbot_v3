SLT "SUWATHA" ANNUAL MEDICAL TESTS SCHEME — 2019

The management of SLT wishes to inform you that SLT Suwatha Program for year 2019 will be commenced from 23rd July 2019. This will be effective till 31st December 2019.

The employees in the relevant age groups are advised to get the medical tests done during the above period. This period will not be extended for any circumstance.

All GMM / DGMM / RTOMM / OPMC Managers and any other managers are requested to send in the attached schedule (Annexure I) hospital wise, filled for employees under their purview to the Medical Section (fax – 011-2329200) at least 04 days prior to the date of requested medical examination.

Based on the employees' requests, the Medical Section will inform the hospital for medical tests on a particular date. The employee has to produce the company Identity Card on entry to the hospital. The employee should bear the cost of any additional tests if performed on his/her request.

These tests can be done for immediate family members and retired employees by confirming identification at the same rates applicable to employees.

SLT Suwatha Program

Instructions

a) You should be fasting for 12-14 consecutive hours to do the Lipid Profile Test. The hospital commences performing tests from 8.00 a.m. accordingly; you have to calculate the hours to start fasting.
b) After doing Lipid Profile, you should not have food, but need to drink water or Ultra Sound Scan.
c) Make sure to take your spectacles and the company ID card along with you.
d) Since the hospital has to get down specialists for medical tests such as 2D Echo & US Scan in abdomen, employees need to reserve the date at least before 4 working days in advance.
e) You are required to call the Medical Section to be confirmed on your appointment before visiting the hospital.
f) These tests under the same subsidized rates can be done for immediate family members and retired employees by paying directly to the hospital on the day of the tests.
