Payment of Cash Advance – April 2020

SLT management is pleased to acknowledge and recognize your determination to maintain our
services in this difficult period, particularly the staff working in the field. Your perseverance,
passion and commitment show once again our ability and resiliency to provide and sustain best
broadband and entertainment experience to our customers. As one team, we quickly reacted to the
crisis situation and adapted to digital means to work from home very effectively regardless of the
time of day or night.

Despite poor collection due to prevailing situation and its impact to company's cash flow, the
Board of Directors have approved a cash advance payment of 3/4 quarter month basic salary in
April 2020 subject to a minimum of LKR 50,000/- for all SLT permanent employees. This cash
advance will be deducted from the year-end annual bonus, which will be decided based on the
company performance.

This cash advance will be paid within next week complying to the other terms and conditions
stipulated in related policy documents of the company.

K A Kiththi Perera
Chief Executive Officer
