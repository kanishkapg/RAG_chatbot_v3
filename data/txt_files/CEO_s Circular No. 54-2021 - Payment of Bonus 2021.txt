CEO's Circular No. 54/2021

CEO's Office, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 01.

02nd December 2021

To: All SLT Employees

PAYMENT OF BONUS - 2021

SLT management is pleased to acknowledge and recognize your commitment to maintain our services in this difficult period. As one team, we quickly reacted to the crisis situation and continue to provide an essential service to the country.

Considering your contributions during the pandemic period and based on organization performances, the Board of Directors have approved a total annual bonus of maximum 4 ½ months basic salary (bonus of 3 ½ fixed number of months basic salary and up to one month performance based bonus of basic salary) for the year 2021 under scenario stipulated hereof, subject to setting off the payment of cash advance paid in April 2021 by SLT.

Payments will be made as shown below;

a) Annual Bonus

| Description of Payments | Payment Scenario |
|---|---|
| Total Bonus Amount    | Maximum up to 4 ½ months basic salary (bonus of 3 ½ fixed number of months basic salary and up to one month performance based bonus of basic salary) subject to a minimum of LKR 150,000/- |
| Method of Payment of Above | Total Bonus    |
| I) Cash Advance Paid in April 2021 | ¾ month basic salary subject to a minimum of LKR 50,000/- |
| II) Balance Amount to be Paid in December 2021 | 2 ¾ months basic salary subject to a minimum of LKR 100,000/- |
| III) Balance Amount to be Paid in February 2022 | Up to one month performance based bonus of basic salary. However the employees whose net sum of 4 ½ months basic salary is less than or equal LKR 150,000/- will not get any additional payment in February 2022. |

General Conditions:

I. SLT Permanent Employees recruited before the year 2021 including the employees who were recruited on contract basis from HCS to SLT (under the intake of June 2020)
Bonus of 3 ½ fixed number of months basic salary and up to one month performance based bonus of basic salary as annual bonus for year 2021 (accordingly total annual bonus of maximum 4 ½ months basic salary) subject to a minimum of LKR 150,000/-.

II. SLT Permanent Employees recruited during the year 2021.
a) Employees who joined SLT between 01.01.2021 - 30.09.2021 will receive a bonus of 3 ½ fixed number of months basic salary and up to one month performance based bonus of basic salary subject to a minimum of LKR 150,000/- on prorate basis.
b) Employees who joined SLT after 01.10.2021 are not entitled to annual bonus.

III. Contract Basis Employees
A payment of festival allowance will be made following the similar criteria of payment of bonus to SLT permanent employees under the section I & II above.

The payment of performance based Bonus of up to 01 month basic salary will be as per the criteria stipulated in the CEO's Circular No. 32/2021 dated 15th June 2021.

b) Ex-gratia Payment

In addition to the above bonus or festival allowance payment of maximum of 04 ½ months basic salary, the Board of Directors have approved to make an ex-gratia payment of LKR 40,000/- to all employees including the contract basis employees as a recognition of the contribution of every employee to run the business without any interruptions during the COVID-19 Pandemic. This is a one-time payment and will not be repeated in the future.

Employees who joined SLT between 01.01.2021 - 30.09.2021 will receive the ex-gratia payment amount of LKR 40,000/- and employees who joined SLT after 01.10.2021 are not entitled to this ex-gratia payment.

Method of Payment

Bonus of 3½ fixed number of months basic salary and the ex-gratia payment of LKR 40,000/- will be paid on or before 10th December 2021 and the balance up to one month performance based bonus of basic salary will be made in the month of February 2022 after completion of performance appraisal process for year 2021. However the employees whose net sum of 4½ months basic salary is less than or equal LKR 150,000/- will not get any additional payment in February 2022.

The amount paid as a bonus in December 2021 will be the bonus after deducting the cash advance payment of 3/4 quarter month basic salary subject to a minimum of LKR 50,000/- paid by SLT in April 2021. All bonus payments will be made in accordance with the bonus payment policy of the company.

I take this opportunity to thank all of you for your significant contribution and dedication during this year. The year ahead will bring its own challenges and opportunities. I am sure with your continuous support and by working together on our priorities and keeping our customers first, we can realize our corporate objectives in the year 2022 as well.

I wish all SLT employees and members of their families a happy & prosperous new year.

K A Kiththi Perera  
Chief Executive Officer
