INTRODUCING A SITE VISIT CHARGE FOR POLE SHIFTING CUSTOMER REQUESTS.

To optimize the operational efficiency by utilizing organizational resources in an effective manner, SLT Management has decided to introduce a Site Visit Charge of Rs. 2000/= for the pole shifting requests made by Customers (excluding Utility Agencies), with immediate effect.

This Site Visit Charge for pole shifting will be charged as an advance payment per request and be deducted from the final estimated value. The account code 224833 (Advances on Subscriber Services) shall be used for the Site Visit Charge.

Any Customer request to shift new pole erections without their consent, which obstruct their existing, access way is excluded from this Site Visit charge.

Guidelines are as shown below.

| No. | Work Description    | Responsible Party |
|---|---|---|
| 1   | Hand over the pole shifting request form (Annexure 01) to the Requester    | RTO/Teleshop    |
| 2   | Once the Requester paid the site visit charge, record the request    | RTO/Teleshop    |
| 3   | Inform the service request to OPMC via email along with the copy of receipt.    | RTO/Teleshop    |
| 4   | Arrange the site visit    | OPMC    |
| 5   | Prepare the Work Order and submit to Job Costing Unit    | OPMC    |
| 6   | Prepare the Estimate and sent to the Requester    | Job Costing Unit    |
| 7   | Once the payment is received, attend the work    | OPMC    |
| 8   | Close the Service Request    | OPMC    |

Note: The above Site Visit Charge is inclusive of government applicable taxes.
