Establishment of “Digital Partner Evaluation Team” (DPET)

As SLT embarks on transforming to a DSP (Digital Service Provider), nurturing of company-wide capabilities in the digital domain is vital, while developing Digital Products and Digital Solutions, targeting customer segments of Enterprise, Government, SME and Consumer. Such digital capabilities will be developed with the formation of a strong and robust Digital Partner Eco-system, which is essential for any DSP to sustain and thrive in this changing business landscape.

In order to realize the objective of building a Digital Partner Eco-System for SLT, the ‘Digital Partner Evaluation Team’ (DPET) is established. The main aim of the DPET is to evaluate the prospective Digital Partners with a view of on-boarding them to SLT’s Digital Partner Eco-system through a streamlined Partner Evaluation Process governed within the realm of the PPIMC (Project Portfolio Investment Management Committee). The Digital Partner Evaluation Team (DPET) will act as the authorized body empowered to Request, Receive, Evaluate and Negotiate Techno-Commercial proposals, thereby acting as a facilitator for the effective delivery of digital capabilities in a highly competitive market environment. In conjunction with the DPET, the PPIMC will be empowered as the authority overseeing the development and delivery of digital capabilities from the view point of Project Portfolio.

Operational details of the newly established “Digital Partner Evaluation Team” (DPET) are included within annexures listed below, which is also posted at ‘Insite→General→Investment Management Framework→DPET’ where subsequent changes to the followings will be made available in the future.

• Annexure (1): DPET Terms of Reference, Composition & High Level Process
• Annexure (2): Amendments to PPIMC to accommodate DPET
• Annexure (3): High Level Interworking between DPET & PPIMC

Accordingly, all relevant officers are hereby requested to adhere to the guidelines and directions given in this circular and its annexures with immediate effect.
