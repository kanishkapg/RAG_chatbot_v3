ENHANCING INVESTMENT GOVERNANCE BY THE ESTABLISHMENT OF “INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT COMMITTEES” [IMCS]

Investment Governance was established at SLT in the year 2009, to decide on what the company’s resources should be spent. Such Investment Governance is essential to ensure the business viability of Investments and delivery of intended values to the business, by assuring that the following five (05) basic criteria are satisfied;

1. Whether the project has captured customer & market requirements satisfactorily.
2. Whether the investment project has a sound business case.
3. Whether we have developed the correct technical solution to encounter 1 and 2 above.
4. Whether the investment risks are managed at various stages of the project.
5. Whether the project delivery management is conducted as per the best practices established.

The existing Investment Governance is reviewed herewith to enhance the management of the company resources by;

- Incorporating Portfolio Management function. [Selecting the right Projects and ensuring an optimum Investment mix for the Programs]
- Incorporating strategic OPEX spent Management to Investment Governance.
- Accommodating latest developments of stage gate process together with Agile Project management practices.

Hence, the new Investment Governance will;

- Govern high value CAPEX investments and OPEX spent at the CEO, COO and appropriate Chief Officer level.
- Establish agility for Investment Management, where by:
  - SLT will prioritize investment projects regularly, for selecting the right projects.
  - SLT will ensure optimum investment mix for programs, considering investment risks and returns and review program level investment regularly.

- Simplify the existing Investment Governance Framework of SLT, by means of:
  - Introducing two new stage-gate routes (namely “Lite” & “Express”), in addition to the existing standard stage-gate route (named as “Full”) to facilitate speedy processing of low risk, high benefit investments and agile projects.

As such, Investment Management Committees [IMCs] are introduced to replace existing Investment Governance Boards [GBs] as follows, with immediate effect with the approval from Board of Directors of SLT.

| Existing ‘Governance Board(GB)’ | New ‘Investment Mgmt. Committee (IMC)’ |
|---|---|
| SGB-Strategic Governance Board    | SIMC-Strategic Investment Mgmt. Committee    |
| PPGB-Project Portfolio Governance Board | PPIMC-Project Portfolio Investment Mgmt. Committee    |
| PGB-Product Governance Board    | PIMC-Product Investment Mgmt. Committee    |
| ESGB-Enterprise Solutions Governance Board | EIMC-Enterprise Solutions Investment Mgmt. Committee    |

In addition, a “Portfolio Management Team” is established to speed up the decision making process of IMCs.

Existing Direct Procurement Committee [DPAC] which was established for the control of inventory materials will be continued to function with the financial limit of less than Rs. 50M.

Operational Expenditure [OPEX] of SLT, except staff related expenses, taxes & levies, utility expenses, statutory and regulatory expenses of cost having individual proposal which is equal or above Rs.25 million shall be approved by SIMC.

Operational details of newly established Investment Management Committees are posted at http://insite.slt.com.lk/Investment%20Management%20Framework/SitePages/Home.aspx Any subsequent changes will be followed in the future.

(b) Enhanced SLT Investment Management Framework

(e) Project Portfolio list categorized as follows:

  - Scalable Governance Process as “Lite”, “Express” and “Full”.
  - Whether financial business case is required or business justification is to be provided.

The expected changes implemented under this circular will enhance the profitability and growth of the company through balanced Portfolio Investments and will ensure that Investment Programs are governed effectively and linked to Customer and Market requirements to maximise the return on investment by taking calculated risks.

Accordingly, this circular will supersede all related circulars issued previously (including Acting CEO’s Circular No: 24/2018) and all relevant officers are hereby requested to adhere to the guidelines and directions given in this circular, with immediate effect.
