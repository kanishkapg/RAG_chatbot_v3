MANAGING ENTERPRISE, GOVERNMENT, CARRIER BUSINESS AND SME CUSTOMER ACCOUNTS IN MINT CRM

By leveraging the capabilities of Mint CRM, the respective Account Manager of Enterprise, Government, Carrier Business and SME customers will be made responsible for managing the entire requirements of the customers.

Accordingly, all the service requests including retail products subscribed by the Enterprise, Government, Carrier Business and SME customers shall be handled by the respective Account Manager

Hence, such sales leads received by the Regional Front Offices shall be directed to the respective Sales Unit with immediate effect.

K A Kiththj-Pérera Chief Executive Officer
