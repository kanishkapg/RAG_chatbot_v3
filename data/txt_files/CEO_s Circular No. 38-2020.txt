AMENDMENT TO THE CEO'S CIRCULAR NO: 69/2019 - SUPERNUMERARY PROMOTION TO RECOGNIZE EMPLOYEES AT THE DATE OF RETIREMENT (DATED 31ST DECEMBER 2019)

Please be informed that the following amendments have been introduced for the prerequisites and eligibility criteria for supernumerary promotion;

1. Amendments to the prerequisites and eligibility criteria

Criteria c. Shall have earned 56 or above points based on the performance grades within maximum of immediate 10 years' period as per the point system detailed in Table 01.

Table 01 - Points for Performance Grading;

| Grading's Before 2016 PF cycle | New grading w.e.f 2016 PF cycle | Points |
|---|---|---|
| S    | O    | 5    | 9    |
| A    | V    | 4    | 6    |
| B, B+   | G    | 3    | 4    |
| C    | N    | 2    | 3    |
| D    | W    | 1    | -1    |
| If the PE grade not available / PE grade not submitted | 0    |    |

Criteria d. Not guilty for any misconduct or pending investigation at the date of the retirement and no unauthorized no pay during the last 10 years.

Other conditions stated in the CEO's Circular No 69/2019 will remain unchanged.

The above conditions & eligibility criteria will be active with immediate effect.
