CEO’s Circular No : 11/ 2019

CEO’s Office, Sri Lanka Telecom, Colombo 01. 
31st January 2019.

To: All Staff

Performance based Incentive Scheme for Service Assurance Teams-2019

In line with organizational objectives in achieving annual business plan of 2019, the management decided to implement an incentive scheme based on the performances achieved by respective teams. This is another endeavor to enhance performance based working culture in SLT. The teams deployed to undertake service assurance activities of OPMCs except cable repair work and rehabilitate work will be enrolled to incentive scheme.

This scheme will contribute to reach higher levels of customer service and satisfaction through a self-motivated work force.

A new operational model will also be established in regions ensuring the clear demarcations and accountabilities to each stake holder. The operational ownership of OSP network related to a specific demarcation will be vested upon the team(s) assigned to particular area(s).

All service assurance teams are entitled to this scheme and incentive payment will be added to following month’s salary starting from February 2019.

All respective supervisors are hereby requested make arrangements to educate relevant staff on this incentive scheme and guide staff to achieve the above successfully. Please also note that necessary incentive schemes are being developed to cover the service fulfillment and service assurance of cable repair and rehabilitation works and this will be announced in the near future.

K A Kififili Perera
Chief Executive Officer
