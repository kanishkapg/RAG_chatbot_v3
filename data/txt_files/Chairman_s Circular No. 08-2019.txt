Chairman's Office
Sri Lanka Telecom PLC
Colombo 1.

17th June, 2019

To : All Employees,

Declaration of Material Management Month

Material Management is a key area of any Business which supports realizing Business Objectives from several perspectives such as Finance, Customer and Operations.

Therefore, SLT Management has identified Material Management as an area where all employees can contribute under "Limitless You" program.

I hereby declare July 2019 as the Material Management month under the "Limitless You" programme expecting a big leap in this area using your potentials.

You are requested to submit your proposals to improve material management on or before 30th June 2019 to sachinis@slt.com.lk

I expect the implementation of identified initiatives, commitment and collaborative efforts of all employees during the Month of Material Management to address all the current issues and to establish a sustainable and effective Material Management System.

Further communication in this regard will be released in future.

P. G. Kumarasinghe Sirisena
Group Chairman
