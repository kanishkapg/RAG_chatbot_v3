CEO's Circular No. 04/2021

CEO's Office, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 01.

22 January 2021

To: All SLT Employees,

SALARY INCREASE FOR THE YEAR 2021

SLT Management is pleased to inform you that the Board of Directors has approved a payment of Annual salary increase of 8.5% for year 2021 to all SLT permanent staff and to the employees who were recruited on contract basis from HCS to SLT (under the intake of June 2020 onwards) with effect from 1st January 2021, subject to the other conditions stipulated in "Annual Salary Revision, Bonus & Leave Encashment Procedure" of the organization. However, employees who have taken unauthorized no-pay leave during the year 2020 will not be entitled for the annual salary increase.

All employees who have joined on contract basis before 1st January 2020 are entitled to receive this benefit based on the terms and conditions of the contract of employment and its renewal.

The management of SLT is grateful on the initiatives taken by all members of the staff in improving productivity and reducing cost during the last year. We look forward to your fullest support in the future as well.

K A Kiththi Perera  
Chief Executive Officer
