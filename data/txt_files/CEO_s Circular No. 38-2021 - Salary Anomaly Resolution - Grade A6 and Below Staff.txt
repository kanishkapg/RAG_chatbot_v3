SALARY ANOMALY RESOLUTION - GRADE A6 AND BELOW STAFF

A series of discussions had been conducted between the management and Trade Unions regarding
salary anomaly correction for the grade A6 to A16 staff in recent past. Several proposals were put
forward for discussion and the management has carefully analyzed all proposals in view of
accommodating the same in line with the organization business performance including the projected
revenue streams anticipated amidst present challenging market conditions, organizations profitability
and other operational KPIs.

Accordingly the management is pleased to offer an upward salary revision to grade A6 to A16 staff as
per following proposal to mitigate salary anomalies highlighted by the Trade Unions and staff with
effect from 1st September 2021.

1. Executive Staff (A6 Grade)

a. 10% salary increase will be provided to all present A6 grade executive staff for grade

promotions from A7 to A6 grade.

b. Accordingly an incremental increase of 4% to basic salary shall be calculated at the point

where the last grade promotion was granted and added to the basic salary with effect from
1st September 2021 (i.e. 6% increase was already granted for A7 to A6 grade promotions
and the proposed salary increase will be applicable only to grade promotions, which has
been effected during year 2006 to date).

2. Non-Executive Staff (A7 to A16 Grades)

a. 6% salary increase will be provided to all present non-executive staff for the grade

promotions since their last promotion date.

b. Accordingly, an incremental increase of 4% to basic salary shall be calculated at the

point where the last grade promotion was granted and added to the basic salary with
effect from 1st September 2021 (i.e. 2% increase was already granted for grade
promotions and the proposed salary increase will be applicable only to one grade
promotion which has been effected during year 2006 to date).

3. HCS staff Recruited to SLT PLC

a. The experience allowance will be increased by 25% and added to the basic salary of the
relevant staff who were recruited from HCS to SLT (including the employees who were
recruited on contract basis from HCS to SLT under the intake of June 2020) as follows.

4. In addition, with respect to the staff who are in grade A6 to A16 and have not received any
grade promotion during the period of 01st January 2006 to 31st December 2015 (excluding the
employees who have already received their grade promotion and covered under section "2"
above), will be offered a 2% salary increase to their basic salary with effect from 01st
September 2021.

The employees who have been recruited to SLT from year 2016 onwards and HCS staff who
were recruited to SLT (excluding the employees who have already received their grade
promotion and covered under section "2" above) will be entitled to get the new revised salary
increase for grade promotions with effect from their next grade promotion. Accordingly 2%
basic salary increase under this scenario will not be applicable to them.

5. All relevant salary adjustments under this proposal will be applicable for the SLT permanent
employees who are in grade A6 to A16 and for the employees who were recruited on contract
basis from HCS to SLT under the intake of June 2020, with effect from 01st September 2021
without back wages.

On the recommendation of the management, SLT Board of Directors have approved the above
proposal as a positive move towards reaching a solution to the outstanding salary anomaly issue.
Notwithstanding the above SLT PLC will continuously offer range of benefits to all staff depending
on the progress of the company and efficient discharge of duties by the staff.

The management extends its appreciation for all trade union representatives for their commitment and
contribution and looks forward for their continuous support for the betterment of the organization.

The management intends to introduce a new Staff Recruitment & Promotion Scheme (SRPS) within the company. Accordingly the management is willing to discuss any other grievances in the future related to employee benefits in line with the new SRPS.

Further the management sincerely appreciates all staff for their support, dedication and commitment amid challenging times to continue SLT's business operations in the context of COVID -19 pandemic.
