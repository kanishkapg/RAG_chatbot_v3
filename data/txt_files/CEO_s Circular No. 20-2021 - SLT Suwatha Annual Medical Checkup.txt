SLT "SUWATHA" ANNUAL MEDICAL CHECKUP

SLT Management is pleased to inform that, the Annual Suwatha Medical Checkup for all permanent employees of SLT will commence from 03.04.2021 to 30.09.2021.

Eligible employees in the relevant age groups are advised to get the medical tests completed during the above period. This period will not be extended for any circumstance.

From this year onwards, all requests are to be submitted online through the link given below. Requests need to be submitted at least 03 working days prior to the date of the medical checkup. Employees can verify the data entered by themselves via the system. Supervisors are deemed to be responsible to inform all the subordinates who do not have email access and all the leave agents are requested to support the employees who do not have the system facilities to enter the details.

Link: INSITE ➔ E-Applications ➔ Employee Relations ➔ SUWATHA Medical Checkup

Or https://employeerelations.sit.com.lk/

Employee Relations Section will inform the relevant hospitals about the employee details for medical tests scheduled on a particular date. The employee has to produce the company Identity Card at the entry to the hospital.

Employee should bear the cost of any additional tests if performed on his/her request.

The employees are advised to get the above tests done on holidays & weekends without causing hindrance to their official duties. However, if any employee wishes to visit the hospital for this purpose on a weekday, he/she needs to obtain the prior approval for availing the own leave and such record must be available in the ERP system before submitting the request.

Special Note:

Please note that, employees shall collect medical reports within two weeks from the relevant hospital. In the event of not collecting the reports from the hospital, the cost of the medical tests will be deducted from their annual indoor medical scheme.

Annexure I

SLT Suwatha Program

Instructions

a) You should fast for 12-14 consecutive hours for the Lipid Profile Test. The hospital commences tests from 8.00 a.m., accordingly, you need to calculate the hours for fasting.

b) After doing Lipid Profile, you should not have food, but need to drink water or king coconut to get the Ultra Sound Scan done.

c) Make sure to take your spectacles and the company ID card along with you.

d) Since the hospital has to arrange specialists for medical tests such as 2D Echo & US Scan in abdomen, employees need to reserve the date at least 3 working days in advance.

e) These tests can be done for immediate family members and retired employees at the same subsidized rates by paying directly to the hospital on the day of the tests.
