CEO's Office, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 01

To All Staff,

ORGANIZATIONAL REARRANGEMENT - REGIONAL GROUP

Please be informed that the following re-arrangement of the Regional Structure will be implemented with immediate effect;

a. Mr. A. Kirupakaran (DCSRO/N&E) will be appointed as the Chief Regional Operations Officer (CROO)

b. Mr. I. B. Wijekoon (CSRO I) will be re-designated as the Chief Regional Sales Officer (CRSO)

c. Centralized Sales Support function will report to Chief Regional Sales Officer.

d. Centralized Operation Support function will report to Chief Regional Operations Officer

e. SLT Contact Centre will be re-arranged under the Marketing Group and Mrs. Y. S. Gunawardena (GM/Contact Centre) shall report to CMO of SLT PLC.

GM - N & E position will be overlooked by Mr. A. Kirupakaran until a suitable replacement is provided.

Organization structure of the Regional Group is detailed as Annexure 01.

We wish them all success in their new job roles.

K. A. Kijuhn Perera
Chief Executive Officer
