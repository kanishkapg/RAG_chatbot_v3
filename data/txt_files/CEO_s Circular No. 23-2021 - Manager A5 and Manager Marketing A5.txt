INTERNAL PROMOTION FOR THE POSTS OF MANAGER AS AND MANAGER (MARKETING) AS

Applications are invited from the permanent, confirmed SLT employees who satisfy the under mentioned requirements to be considered for the posts of Manager A5 and Manager (Marketing) A5.

A. ELIGIBILITY

Three (03) years' experience in A6 Grade as a Senior Executive Assistant Manager / Senior Executive Assistant Manager (Marketing) A5 in SLT as at \(3^{1^8}\) March 2021.

B. SELECTION METHOD

1. Selection will be made through an examination and an interview.
2. Examination will be held in English Medium.
3. Interview will be held only for successful candidates of the examination for the final selection.

The subjects specified for the examination are as follows:
i) SLT Procedures, Practices & Business Affairs (40 minutes)
ii) Subject Knowledge
- Part I - Multiple Choice Questions (45 minutes)
- Part II - Essay Type Questions (1 \% hours)

C. PLACEMENT

Selected candidates will be promoted as Manager A5 Grade / Manager (Marketing) A5 Grade and placed in the vacant positions.

D. METHOD OF APPLYING

Eligible candidates should fill in the attached application form and forward to GM/ HR & IR (Acting), \(7^{th}\) Floor, HQ Building, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Lotus Road, Colombo 01 through the relevant General Manager / Deputy General Manager.

E. CLOSING DATE

Applications should be sent within seven (07) working days of issuing this circular. Applications sent after the closing date will not be considered.
