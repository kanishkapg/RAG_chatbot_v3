To All Staff:

ASSIGNING RESPONSIBILITIES TO GUIDE AND ASSIST IN IMPLEMENTING INVENTORY MANAGEMENT BEST PRACTICES

Proper inventory management saves money and time while helping to increase customer satisfaction by serving them on time. In the recent Stores Verification it is noted that there is a tremendous improvement in Main Store and Provincial Store. However, when we analyze the whole inventory it is noted that we have to improve the attention we pay for inventory management related to certain other stores and locators.

To guide and assist in establishing and maintaining the best inventory management practices in the whole organization it is decided to assign following officers.

| Area    | Responsible Officers |
|---|---|
| Provincial stores and all associated locators | Provincial Accountant together with stores officer of respective province |
| Special Stores IT/PABX/P&AC etc. and associated locators | Accountant/FA together with stores officer of the Special Store |
| Contractor Stores    | DGM/TNW Projects, DGM/FA, DGM/IM with their officers responsible for inventory management |

The owner of each regional locator is responsible to update the material usage with the help of WFM & ERP. The other locator owners attached to special stores who are not using WFM is responsible to keep the information related to his locator updated all the time on ERP. The Owner of each Locator is accountable for implementing the best Inventory Management Practices and maintaining updated locator information on ERP and they have to escalate any issue directly to above officers related to the area.

Following are the responsibilities of the above assigned officers:

(1) Guide locator owners on inventory management practices of SLT.
(2) Communicate decisions taken related to inventory management to locator owners & other related officers in their service area.
(3) Conduct random audits in one third of total locators in the responsible area each moth & send reports to DGM/FA & DGM/IM so that at least every locator is audited once in 03 month.
(4) Direct the locator owners for training to improve the required knowledge, skills & attitude.
(5) Escalate repetitive non compliances to GM/FA for further actions.
(6) Seek the assistance of Inventory Management Section, Fixed Asset Section to address the issues.
(7) Escalate issues which need the process or other changes to improve inventory management practices.

The active participation of the assigned officers and the close coordination of locator owners are expected to improve inventory management practices of SLT. The General Managers and Deputy General Managers of respective areas are expected to monitor and ensure the implementation of these guidelines.
