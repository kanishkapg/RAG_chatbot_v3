INFORMATION SECURITY VIOLATIONS

SLT is fully committed to the principles of integrity, fairness, accountability, confidentiality and disclosures in all its business dealings and activities, and ensuring a safe and healthy work environment. “SLT Code of Business Practice” is therefore put together as a guiding principle to all employees (permanent, contract or on secondment) and Directors of SLT and its subsidiaries on how they should conduct themselves while in the service of the Company.

Whilst the company is committed to provide a safe and conductive working environment, it is the responsibility of every employee to understand and comply with the company’s code of business practices. Therefore, all the employees are hereby requested to strictly comply with following Information security guidelines to avoid violations on company’s information security.

- Employees must safeguard all confidential information by labeling information as per the SLT information classification standards, keeping it secure and limiting access to those who have a need to know in order to carry out their jobs.
- Where Company confidential information needs to be disclosed to external persons, a non-disclosure agreement or equivalent agreement shall be entered into by the parties to ensure that the confidential information is protected.
- An employee must continue to keep Company information confidential despite the termination of the employee’s contract of employment.
- Use of email and access to internet must be solely for business purposes. Material from the internet or email that are or could be perceived as offensive in terms of religion, politics, ethnicity or gender or which may potentially incite hatred or ill feelings, are prohibited from being received, forwarded or sent.

- Employees are not to publish, post or release any materials and/or participate in any forums that might harm the Company’s reputation and/or potentially create a hostile working environment, and/or release of any information on any social media that is considered confidential whether it is done on Company provided equipment/devices or otherwise and/or whether it is done during or after working hours.

For further reference, the Code of Business Practice can be downloaded from: Intranet > Group > CHRO > Human Resource> HR Strategies & Policy> Policies> HR Policies> SLT Code of Ethics.

All the employees are required at all times to comply with all applicable laws and regulations and perceived pressures or demands from other employees or business conditions are not excuses for violating the information security or this Code of Business Practice.
