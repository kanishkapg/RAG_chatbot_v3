ORGANIZATION RE-ARRANGEMENT

In view of carrying out investigations and other inquiries independently and a transparent manner, it was decided to transfer the investigation unit of SLT to GCIA with immediate effect.

Due to the above change, following reporting structure will be made with immediate effect.
