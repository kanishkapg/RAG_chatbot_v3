PERFORMANCE BASED REWARD SYSTEM TO ENCOURAGE EMPLOYEES FOR PERFORMANCE BASED WORKING CULTURE  

To enhance performance based working culture in the company, the management has decided to implement performance based reward system more effectively from next year (2020) onwards. Management expects the system will contribute to reach higher level of customer service and satisfaction through a self-motivated work force which will always help to keep in line with achieving the organization objectives and its annual business plan.  

Accordingly, to recognize the commitment of employees towards a highly performing organization, it has been decided to consider individual performance of each employee for employee rewards - particularly for the payments such as annual bonus and annual salary increase. This will be done by assessing the performance of employee in the prevailing year as per the given targets and objectives. The management has done few changes in view of further improving SLT Employee Performance Management system. It is important to have feedback not only from their immediate supervisors but also for their teamwork contributions for accomplishing multiple tasks and projects handled by different departments. This would give a better picture of employee contribution. Further all supervisory officers must ensure establishment of individual targets with clear demarcation and accountabilities to each employee.  

This will be implemented after having discussions with trade unions and taking their inputs in view of developing a reliable, transparent and fair system.  

K A Kiththi-Perera  
Chief Executive Officer
