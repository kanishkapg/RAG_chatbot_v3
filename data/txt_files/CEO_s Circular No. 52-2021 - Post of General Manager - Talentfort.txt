CEO's Circular No: 52/2021

CEO's Office, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 01.

2nd November 2021

To: All General Managers & Deputy General Managers,

POST OF GENERAL MANAGER – TALENTFORT

Talentfort (Pvt) Ltd. is a subsidiary company of HCS Pvt. Ltd. operated under SLT Group of companies. To streamline operations and to focus on accelerated business growth the above vacancy is advertised internal to SLT PLC.

Applications are invited from the present General Managers/Deputy General Managers who satisfy the under mentioned requirements to be considered for the above post.

1. ELIGIBILITY FOR TRANSFER
GMs (A1, A2 & A3) in Engineering / Accounting / Marketing professions who wish to be transferred to above position shall apply.

2. ELIGIBILITY FOR PROMOTION

2.1 FOR DGMs PRESENTLY IN A2 GRADE:
DGMs (A2) in Engineering / Accounting / Marketing Professions who wish to be considered for the above position shall apply.

2.2 FOR DGMs PRESENTLY IN A3 GRADE:
(a) Engineering Profession
(i) Corporate Membership of IESL/IET (IEE) UK/MechE (UK)/Institution of Civil Engineers (UK) with 5 years' experience in A3 Grade in SLT.

(b) Accounting Profession
(i) Associate Membership of ICA/CIMA/ACCA with 5 years' experience in A3 Grade in SLT.

(c) Marketing Profession
(i) Membership (MCIM) of CIM with Chartered Marketer status or equivalent qualification with 5 years' experience in A3 Grade in SLT.

2.3 FOR DGMs PRESENTLY IN A4 GRADE:
(a) Engineering Profession
(i) Corporate Membership of IESL/IET (IEE) UK/ IMechE (UK)/Institution of Civil Engineers (UK) with 3 years engineering experience in A4 Grade in SLT.
OR
(ii) An Engineering Degree/Associate Membership of IESL/IET (IEE) UK/MechE (UK)/Institution of Civil Engineers (UK) with 5 years engineering experience in A4 Grade in SLT.
OR
(iii) 7 years engineering experience in A4 Grade in SLT.

(b) Accounting Profession
(i) Associate Membership of ICA/CIMA/ACCA with 3 years accounting/auditing experience in A4 Grade in SLT.
OR
(ii) Intermediate qualification of ICA/CIMA/ACCA with 5 years accounting/auditing experience in A4 Grade in SLT.
OR
(iii) 7 years accounting / auditing experience in A4 Grade in SLT.

(c) Marketing Profession
(i) Membership (MCIM) of CIM with Chartered Marketer status or equivalent qualification with 3 years' experience in marketing in A4 Grade in SLT.
OR
(ii) Associate Graduate Membership (ACIM) of CIM or equivalent qualification with 5 years' experience in marketing in A4 Grade in SLT.
OR
(iii) 7 years marketing experience in A4 Grade in SLT.

2.4 Deputy General Managers in A3 Grade who do not possess required number of years' experience in A3 Grade under category 2.2 will be eligible under category 2.3 by adding the number of years' experience in A4 Grade and A3 Grade together and those officers may also apply.

3. SELECTION METHOD
Selection will be by an interview.

4. PLACEMENT
(a). Transfer (As per Section 1)
Selected General Manager will be seconded to Talentfort (Pvt) Ltd as General Manager-Talentfort.

(b). Promotion (As per Section 2)
Selected candidate will be promoted to General Manager position (i.e. initial appointment will be in Acting capacity) as per the provisions available in the SRPS and thereafter will be seconded to Talentfort (Pvt) Ltd as General Manager-Talentfort.

Subsequent to serving in the Acting capacity for 06 months' period, if the selected officer is in A3 Grade, he /she will be placed in A2 Grade and if the selected officer is in A4 Grade he/she will be placed in A3 Grade. Further, if the selected officer is in A2 Grade, his/her Grade will remain unchanged.

5. METHOD OF APPLYING
Eligible candidates should fill in the attached application form and forward to GM/HR & IR through the relevant Supervising Officer.

6. CLOSING DATE
Applications should be sent within 07 days of issuing this Circular.
