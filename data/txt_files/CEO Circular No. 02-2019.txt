ANNUAL PERFORMANCE APPRAISAL - 2018

Please be informed that the annual performance appraisals for 2018 should be started with effect from 5th January 2019 and the effective duration should be for the period from 01st January 2018 to 31st December 2018. Managing staff performance is a significant supervisory responsibility. Because it directly enables staff members to perform effectively to increase the departmental and company performance.

“Please note that all supervising officers are responsible on ensuring the completion of effective performance appraisal for all their subordinates and also need to complete their subordinators’ appraisal before the participating of their own appraisal interview.”

Performance Appraisal
Appraisal of all A10 and above staff (A1 to A10) should be completed through ERP system. Appraisal of A11 and below (A11 to A16) staff, including security staff should be completed using manual form available in the intranet.

Performance Appraisal Process
Appraisal process consists of 3 steps, such as self-appraisal (appraisee), supervisor appraisal (appraiser) and reviewer appraisal. Completed appraisal should be reached to DGM-HR Strategies & Policy through relevant protocol with the approval of respective Chief Officer on or before the dates specified in the below time plan.

Performance Appraisal Interview
It is compulsory to conduct a year-end performance appraisal interview between appraisee, appraiser and reviewer to exchange first-hand informations which will form as part of their overall assessment. This interview duration should be not less than 15 minutes. During the interview all the parties (Appraisee, Appraiser, and Reviewer) must share their views on levels of targets achievements, competencies, gaps and areas to be improved for the next year. It is also necessary to communicate the relevant appraisal rating to the employee with feedback.

Performance Appraisal Guidelines
The following guideline documents and forms can be downloaded through HR website.
• Performance Management Policy & Process.
• Performance Appraisal Form applicable to A11 and below staff.
• Performance Appraisal ERP User Manual.

Please read the complete Management Policy & Process document and ERP User Manual prior to the commencement of the appraisal. All Executive Supervising Officers are requested to educate their subordinates on the performance appraisal policies to facilitate the evaluation. Also note our HR Coordinators will assist you throughout the performance appraisal process.

(Above documents can be downloaded from: Intranet>Group>CHRO>Human Resource>HR Strategies & Policy> Performance Management>Performance Management.)

Performance Based Training
To achieve the ultimate objective of developing employees’ performances, it is necessary to have an appropriate training. Right training support employee to improve their competencies and provide the benefits of achieving the desirable level in performance.

Supervising Officers can recommend the relevant training for their subordinates via ERP. Training programs which are not scheduled in the ERP can be requested by using separate training application form available in the intranet. Please refer performance appraisal ERP user manual for more information.

Time plan
• 15th January 2019 (before) - Completion of self-appraisal.
• 20th January 2019 (before) - Completion of appraiser-appraisal.
• 31st January 2019 (on or before) - Completion of reviewer-appraisal
• 10th February 2019 (on or before) - HR will provide appraisal summary report to each DGM for their approval
• 28th February 2019 (on or before) - Sending approved list of final ratings to the HR (to DGM-HR Strategies & Policy)
