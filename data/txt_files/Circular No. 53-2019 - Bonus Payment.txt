CEO's Circular No. 53 /2019

CEO's Office
Sri Lanka Telecom PLC
Colombo 01
14 November 2019

To: All SLT Employees,

PAYMENT OF BONUS - 2019

The Management of SLT is grateful on the progress as well as the initiatives taken by all members of the staff in achieving company ABP targets during the year. The Board having considered the importance of reinforcing a strong relationship between Employees and Management approved 4 months' basic salary and LKR 25,000.00 as payment of bonus for the year 2019. Payment will be done as shown below;

I. All SLT Permanent Employees
Total Bonus of 4 months' basic salary and LKR 25,000.00 subject to a minimum of LKR 100,000.00.

II. Permanent Employees Recruited During the Year 2019.
a) Employees who joined SLT between 01.01.2019 - 31.10.2019 will receive a bonus of 4 months' basic salary and LKR 25,000.00 subject to a minimum of LKR 100,000.00 on proate basis.
b) Employees who joined SLT after 01.11.2019 are not entitled to annual bonus.

III. Contract Basis Employees
An ex-gratia payment will be paid following the similar criteria of payment of bonus to SLT permanent employees under the section I & II above.

The bonus will be paid during the first week of December 2019 and amount paid as bonus in December 2019 will be the net bonus after deducting the advance bonus of 1 ¼ months' salary (1 ¼ months' salary or one month plus LKR 25,000.00) already paid in April 2019 and the special allowance of LKR 25,000.00 already paid in January 2019. All payments will be made in accordance with the bonus payment policy of the company.

I take this opportunity to thank all of you for your significant contribution and dedication during this year. The year ahead will bring its own challenges and opportunities. I am sure with your continuous support while keeping focused on our priorities, and putting our customers first, we can realize our corporate objectives in the year 2020 as well.

I wish all SLT employees and members of their families a Very Happy & Prosperous New Year.

K A Kiththi Berera
Chief Executive Officer
