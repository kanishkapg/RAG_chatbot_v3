IMPLEMENTATION OF ANNUAL BUSINESS PLAN 2019

As per Chairman's Circular 07/2019 dated 17th June 2019, new RTO and OPMC areas have been established in rapidly developing urbanize cities to increase SLT's customer reach and to improve market penetration in an accelerated pace.

Further the management has decided not to establish said RTO/OPMC's physically at the moment. However, it shall require to reach customers and to improve market penetration of said rapidly developing urbanize cities. Hence it was proposed to operationalize following new RTO/OPMCs virtually managed by existing RTOM and M-OPMCs as below.

Accordingly, RTOM, and M-OPMCs who are assigned with covering up duties are responsible for achievement of Sales and Operational KPIs of respective new RTO and OPMCs.

| RTO/OPMC    | Covering-up by    |
|---|---|
|    | RTOM    | M-OPMC    |
| Maharagama    | Nugegoda    | Nugegoda    |
| Piliyandala    | Rathmalana    | Rathmalana    |
| Bandaragama    | Horana    | Panadura    |
| Horowpathana    | Anuradhapura    | Anuradhapura    |

Above arrangement will be effective from 8th July 2019 onwards.

Area demarcations of RTOs of WPSE, WPSW, WPS, NC provinces and Regional Enterprise Business in WPC province stipulated in Chairman's Circular 07/2019 will remain unchanged.

P G Kumarasinghe Sirisena
