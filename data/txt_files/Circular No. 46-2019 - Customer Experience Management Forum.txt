CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE MANAGEMENT FORUM  

SLT management believe and recognized Customer Experience Management as an important activity. SLT strongly believes customer experience as our key business differentiator to regain the market leadership and all employees' contribution and commitment is expected to improve our customer experience to drive towards the market leadership in telecommunication industry.  

Further to strengthen the customer experience activity, a forum is established under the GCEO circular no. 20/2016. At this meeting senior management will take up and expedite execution of company-wide initiatives that will make SLT as the best customer experience provider in the telecommunication industry. By doing this activity SLT expect to provide a superior customer service to our valued customers and thrive in the SLT business objectives.  

Customer Experience Management Forum (CEM Forum) will meet every quarter. Following officers are appointed to the steering committee on permanent basis. Committee will request assistance of other officers as and when required.
