CEO's Circular No: 28/2020

CEO's Office, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 01.

04 June 2020

To All Staff,

APPOINTMENT OF CHIEF PEOPLE OFFICER

Please be informed that Mr. Aruna Jayasekera will be appointed as the Chief People Officer of SLT PLC with effect from 1st July 2020. He will be responsible for the Strategic Human Resource Management, Industrial Relations and Talent Development functions of the organization.

Mr. Jayasekera is a fellow member of the Chartered Institute of Personnel Management Sri Lanka and an experienced HR professional with a progressive career spanning over 25 years in the financial and export manufacturing sectors. Further he holds a Bachelor of Laws (LLB) from the Faculty of Law, University of Colombo and is an Attorney-at-Law.

Mr.Jayasekera commenced his professional career as a State Counsel at the Attorney General's Department, before changing to Human Resources Management. He served as the Regional HR Manager (Manufacturing) Asia Pacific for Anseli Ltd and worked as the Group Chief Human Resources Officer at Sampath Bank PLC prior to joining SLT PLC.

On behalf of SLT PLC, I request your fullest corporation to carry out his duties and wish him all success in the new role.

K. A. Kiththi-Perera
Chief Executive Officer
