CEO's Circular No. 18/2021

CEO's Office, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 01.

31 March 2021

To: All SLT Staff,

PAYMENT OF CASH ADVANCE - APRIL 2021

SLT management is pleased to acknowledge and recognize your determination, passion and commitment to manage our services in this difficult period while providing the best experience to our customers.

I'm pleased to inform that the Board of Directors have approved a cash advance payment of 3/4 month's basic salary in April 2021 subject to a minimum of LKR 50,000/- as an advance bonus payment to all SLT permanent staff. This cash advance will be deducted from the year-end annual bonus, which will be decided based on the performance of the company and individual employee performance. This cash advance will be paid during the first week of April 2021 in complying with the other terms and conditions stipulated in related policy documents of the company.

I take this opportunity to thank all of you for your significant contribution and dedication during this year. I'm confident that with your continuous support and by working together, keeping focused on our priorities and putting our customers first, we can realize organizational corporate objectives in the year 2021.

I wish all SLT employees and members of their families a happy and prosperous Sinhala and Hindu New Year.

K A Ki thrfi Perera
Chief Executive Officer
