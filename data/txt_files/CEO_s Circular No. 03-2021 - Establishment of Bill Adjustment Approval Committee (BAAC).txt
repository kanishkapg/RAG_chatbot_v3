| Adjustment Amount | Recommendation | Approval |
|---|---|---|
| Upto Rs.50,000.00    | Assistant Manager    | Accountant/CRC    |
| Between Rs.50,001.00 and Rs.100,000.00 | Accountant/CRC    | DGM/OS    |

| Over Rs.1Million    | BAAC Committee and CFO    | CEO    |
