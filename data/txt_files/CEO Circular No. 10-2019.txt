IMPLEMENTATION OF ABP 2019

EMPOWERING HUMAN RESOURCES MANAGEMENT (HRM) FUNCTION AND DELEGATION OF HRM AUTHORITIES

With the view of empowering core functional heads on managing their staff, management has decided to decentralize the following HR operational activities with effect from 1st February 2019.

1. Management of Daily Attendance
CXO/ DCXO/ GMM/ DGMM/ RTOMM/ M-OPMC are empowered and they are responsible to reconcile and validate daily attendance of respective staff under them based on the information received from the attendance system & to verify the physical presence. Employee leave and absenteeism has to be properly managed, maintained and all staff movements need to be properly updated and maintained with a movement register (According to the CHRO’s Circular 01/2019). They have to submit e-reports to CHRO monthly.
Further,
i. Leave of employees should be recommended by immediate supervisor & approved by CXO/ DCXO/ GMM/ DGMM/ RTOMM/ M-OPMC.
ii. No show, unauthorized leave, absenteeism, non-visibility should be informed to HR division immediately for each and every individual separately.
iii. Movement register
    • One movement register per office should be maintained in addition to ERP system.
    • Movement should be recommended by supervising officer & approved by CXO/ DCXO/ GMM/ DGMM/ RTOMM/ M-OPMC

2. Staff Cost Analysis & Reporting
CXO/ DCXO/ GMM/ DGMM/ RTOMM/ M-OPMC should maintained their employee costs as per the Annual Business Plan and proper staff cost management should be done based on assigned duties of the employees. They are responsible for entire staff cost management of the respective unit. They should be aware of their staff cost indicator and should be updated every month.

3. Certification, Recommendations and Approval of Additional Working hours
Recommendation on Over Time (OT) allocation should be done by the relevant supervising officer and approval to be done by the relevant CXO/ DCXO/ GMM/ DGMM/ RTOMM/ M-OPMC. Approving officers are responsible for work accomplishment as against the approved OT hours.

4. Performance Appraisal And Incentive Management
CXO/ DCXO/ GMM/ DGMM/ RTOMM/ M-OPMC are responsible for managing incentive payments and for the following activities under performance management,

- Develop Roles and Responsibilities for employees in respective Group/Division/Section or Unit and preparing Job Descriptions accordingly.
- Assigning Targets for Employees.
- Continuous Monitoring of Achievements of the Targets.
- Evaluations of individual’s performances mainly based on achievements.
- Gap analysis and development of staff.

5. Relevant Cost Management Based On Activity Based Costing System
CXO/ DCXO/ GMM/ DGMM/ RTOMM/ M-OPMC are responsible for entire cost management of the respective Group/Division/Section or Unit.

6. Unit Rate Management (Applicable To HCS Staff)
Cost allocated based on Unit Rates for management of HCS staff has to be maintained at each unit and available for P& L accounting.

7. Activity Based Cost Management, Management Accounting And Profit Center Based Cost Management System
CXO/ DCXO/ GMM/ DGMM/ RTOMM/ M-OPMC are accountable for the above cost management system.

8. Work Place Behavior Management
CXO/ DCXO/ GMM/ DGMM/ RTOMM/ M-OPMC are empowered to take necessary action specially on following behaviors subjects to company policy on such behavior.

- Un-authorized absence/Absenteeism.
- Breach of Information Security policy.

9. Preliminary Inquiries
Managers or officers above the managers’ level are authorized to initiate preliminary inquiries against any kind of misconduct according to the company’s disciplinary procedures. Such investigation reports should be submitted to CHRO for further actions.

10. Following Functions are Excluded From Above

- Recruitment
- Promotions or Demotions
- Salary Increments
- Service Extension
- Auditing Investigations and formal inquiries
- Employee Termination

11. Transfers
Authority will be delegated to CXO/ DCXO/ GMM/ DGMM/ RTOMM/ M-OPMC to implement staff transfers within the Group, Division, Region, Section, Province or Unit. DGM-HR Operation should be informed to update the ERP System.

12. Limits and Authority Levels.
CXO/ DCXO/ GMM/ DGMM/ RTOMM/ M-OPMC are authorized for implementing the above functions.

All correspondence referred to CHRO for reference and to update employee’s personal file.
