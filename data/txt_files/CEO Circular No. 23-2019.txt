DECLARATION OF SLT TOOLS WEEK

Main purpose of the tools week is to educate all SLT field staff on the importance of using the proper tools in their day to day activities. The activities planned during the week will emphasize on the importance of using the correct tool to execute the job in a professional manner.

Use of right tool with right expertise will ensure good workmanship and this will lead to improve the efficiency with a quality work, which will minimize repeated faults.

This exercise will improve the brand image of SLT which will also help to reduce the customer churn.

SLT top management has decided to declare the tools week from 22nd to 29th May 2019 as one of its key corporate events. Further 22nd May, 2019 is declared as Tools Verification Day to ensure the tools inventory, quality of tools and shortages of tools.

This program is executed as a part of “ASEEMITHA OBA” Program in view of creating a better working environment for our employees.

Your participation and commitment are highly appreciated in order to make this event a great success.

K A Kiththi Pefera
Chief Executive Officer
