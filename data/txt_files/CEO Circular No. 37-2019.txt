ARCHIVING OF SLT CUSTOMER RELATED DOCUMENTS.

In terms of Electronic Transactions Act No. 19 of 2006 documents stored in Electronic Form are admissible in law. Accordingly, SLT can form Agreements / Contracts on line and the related correspondence which could be archived in electronic mode.

Further the existing Customer Agreements and the other related correspondence/records to the respective transaction could be stored in an electronically archived mode. Hence there will be no requirement to maintain the related Documents in hardcopy format other than in the following instances:
a. Any legal requirement arising due to an ongoing inquiry or Legal Proceeding.
b. Documents excluded from the application of the Electronics Transactions Act.
c. Any SLT internal requirements.

The Electronic Transactions Act specifically excludes the following documents from its application. Therefore, the originals of these documents require to be retained by SLT in its hard format.

1. Notarial documents (Deeds, Lease Agreements etc.), Instruments of Trust and Powers of Attorney.
2. Cheques, Bills of exchange and bank documents.
3. Licenses / Certificates (including Birth, Death, Marriage Certificates.).
4. Contracts for sale of immovable property.
5. Financial documents on regulated exchange, foreign exchange transactions, interbank payments, securities or financial assets.
6. Loan Agreements and Transfer of Security Rights.

Right to Information Act (RTI Act) requires providing of information by Public Authorities when required. However, any information maintained under the RTI Act can be kept in Electronic form. (however subject to the instances mentioned under (a), (b) and (c) above).

As per the RTI Act the information should be kept for the stipulated period of 12 years. Accordingly, the Documents relating to customer information needs to kept for the entire period of provisioning of services to a particular customer and further period of 12 years. However, the Billing Information can be kept for a period of 12 years from the date of its origination. (subject to (a), (b) and (c) above)

If any Information owner is satisfied that any Document cannot be converted to Electronic Form or are not necessary to be maintained for the above referred, the Information owner may decide and discard the information accordingly (subject to (a), (b) and (c) above).
