4. SLT Vision Com Payments

CEO of SLTVC shall be given the authority equivalent to the level of SLT Chief Officer to contract with the content providers under the conditions of the authority matrix, if CEO of SLTVC is an employee of SLT. Contracts with the local content providers for Video on Demand can also be signed by CEO of SLTVC.

5. The above authorisation limits are including Taxes and Levies except VAT as long as VAT is recoverable.

6. The officer approving the payments/budgets and signing the agreements is responsible to strictly adhere to the authorization limits stated above.
