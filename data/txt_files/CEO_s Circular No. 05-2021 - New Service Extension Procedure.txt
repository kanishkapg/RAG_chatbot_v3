To: All SLT Employees,

NEW SERVICE EXTENSION PROCEDURE

In relation to the current proposal by the Government to extend the employment retirement age to 60 years, approval for the same has been debated in Parliament of Sri Lanka and related agencies. Either this statute will become accepted or not, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC recognizes the strength of its employees and their experience as an asset to the company and make every endeavor to accommodate service extensions of SLT staff beyond 55 years of age in line with a well-established and transparent procedure.

Further, service extensions of SLT employees will be assessed by the management solely based on the future business requirements and individual basis, taking into account the individual's willingness, performance, conduct, discipline and skills in the anticipated business environment.

To streamline and strengthen the service extension procedure of the company, following amendments will be introduced with immediate effect.

a. Employee retirement will be attended in accordance with Sri Lankan Labour Law while retaining the option for the company to continue with employment of willing employees where it is determined to be in the Company's interest.

b. Employees who have fulfilled required eligibility criteria shall receive a request for consent related to their service extensions by the management, three (03) months prior to an employee reaching the age of 55 years/end of the employee's existing service extension period. Employee is required to indicate his/her willingness to the management for a further service extension.

c. Previously the service extension evaluation was de-centralized and conducted by relevant line managers. As per the new procedure, the service extension evaluation shall be conducted in a transparent manner by a centralized committee.

d. An opportunity to appeal in the event of a dispute in extension will be introduced with the formation of an appeal committee under the new procedures.

The New Service Extension Procedure (Document No: HSPS/SEP/018) is available at SLT Intranet [General>SLT Common Document Portal>Policies>Human Resources (CPO)>Service Extension Procedure]

As done in the past, all service extensions will be submitted for sanction of the Board of Directors. Above procedure shall supersede previous procedures/circulars/policies/instructions/ internal memos, which describe the same subject contents.
