INTRODUCTION OF REFERRAL PAYMENT SCHEME FOR EXTERNAL COURSES CONDUCTED BY SLT TALENT DEVELOPMENT CENTERS

Internationally recognized programs are conducted at SLT Talent Development Centers through affiliations with reputed qualifying bodies such as University of Hertfordshire - UK, Pearson - UK etc., with the objective of optimum utilization of available resources while sharing the experience and knowledge of our staff with general public.

The Management has decided to offer an opportunity for employees of SLT, to introduce students to courses conducted at SLT Talent Development Centers and earn a referral fee. Upon successful enrollment of student to the course, the referral payment will be made to the relevant staff member as described below;
