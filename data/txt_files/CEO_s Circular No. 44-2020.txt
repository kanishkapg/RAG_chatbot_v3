CEO's Office, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 01
17 September 2020

To All General Managers, Deputy General Managers,

POST OF GENERAL MANAGER – SLT DIGITAL INFO SERVICES

SLT Digital Info Services Pvt Ltd is a subsidiary company operated under SLT Group of companies. To streamline the operations as per SLT strategic priorities, the above vacancy is advertised internal to SLT PLC.

Applications are invited from the permanent Deputy General Managers of A2, A3 and A4 grades of SLT, who satisfy the under mentioned requirements and selected candidate will be promoted to General Manager position as per the provisions available in the SRPS and thereafter will be seconded to SLT Digital Info Services Pvt Ltd as GM-SLT Digital Info Services.

1. ELIGIBILITY

1.1 FOR OFFICERS PRESENTLY IN A3 GRADE:

(a) Engineering Profession

(i) Corporate Membership of IESL/IEE(UK)/IMech E(UK)/Institution of Civil Engineers (UK) with 5 years' experience in A3 Grade in SLT

(b) Accounting Profession

(i) Associate Membership of ICA/CIMA/ACCA with 5 years' experience in A3 Grade in SLT

1.2 FOR OFFICERS PRESENTLY IN A4 GRADE:

(a) Engineering Profession

(i) Corporate Membership of IESL/IEE(UK)/IMech E(UK)/Institution of Civil Engineers(UK) with 3 years engineering experience in A4 Grade in SLT OR

(ii) An Engineering Degree/Associate Membership of IESL / IEE(UK) / IMech E(UK) / Institution of Civil Engineers(UK) with 5 years engineering experience in A4 Grade in SLT OR

(iii) 7 years engineering experience in A4 Grade in SLT

(b) Accounting Profession
(i) Associate Membership of ICA/CIMA/ACCA with 3 years accounting / auditing experience in A4 Grade in SLT OR
(ii) Intermediate qualification of ICA/CIMA/ACCA with 5 years accounting / auditing experience in A4 Grade in SLT OR
(iii) 7 years accounting / auditing experience in A4 Grade in SLT

1.3 Deputy General Managers in A3 Grade who do not possess required number of years' experience in A3 Grade under category 1.1 will be eligible under category 1.2 by adding the number of years' experience in A4 Grade and A3 Grade together and those officers may also apply.

2. OPPORTUNITY FOR PRESENT GMs & DGMs IN A2 GRADE AGMs (A2/A3) and DGMs (A2) in engineering and accounting professions who wish to be considered for the above position may also apply.

3. SELECTION METHOD Selection will be by an interview.

4. PLACEMENT Selected candidate will be initially appointed in Acting capacity. Subsequent to serving in the acting capacity for 06 months' period, if the selected officer is in A3 Grade, he /she will be placed in A2 Grade and if the selected officer is in A4 Grade he / she will be placed in A3 Grade. Further, if the selected officer is in A2 Grade, his/her Grade will remain unchanged.

5. METHOD OF APPLYING Eligible candidates should fill in the attached application form and forward to DGM-HR Operations through the relevant Supervising Officer.

6. CLOSING DATE Applications should be sent within 07 days of issuing this circular.

K. A. Kiththi Pefera Chief Executive Officer
