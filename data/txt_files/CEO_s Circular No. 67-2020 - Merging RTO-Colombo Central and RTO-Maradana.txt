CEO’s Circular No.67 / 2020

CEO’s Office, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 01.

17 December 2020

To: All SLT Employees,

MERGING RTO/COLOMBO CENTRAL AND RTO/MARADANA

Please be informed that RTO/Colombo Central and RTO/Maradana will be merged and operated as a single entity with effect from 1st January 2021.

This will provide opportunity to utilize combined strength of the staff in the two regional offices, while facilitating optimum use of resources. Further, this move will pave the way to broader the area to carry out sales activities, thus leaving the avenues open for a better growth in the customer base.

Subsequent to the above merger, the resulting Regional Telecom Office will be named as “Regional Telecom Office – Maradana” and Mr. Rukshan Edirisinghe will be appointed as the RTOM/Maradana.

Please note that, Regional Telecom Office – Colombo Central will be ceased to exist with effect from 31st December 2020.

K. A. Kithibi Perera
Chief Executive Officer
