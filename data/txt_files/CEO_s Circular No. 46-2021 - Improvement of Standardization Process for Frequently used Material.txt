IMPROVEMENT OF STANDARDIZATION PROCESS FOR FREQUENTLY USED MATERIAL

CEO's Circular on Standardization of Frequently Used Material (Circular No 14/2021) is amended as follows to introduce the process to be followed by the Standardization Committees for approving material samples received outside the tendering process. This amendment facilitates to accommodate more advanced material at competitive prices to SLT network and to eliminate material shortages and scarcities due to issues in the market.

- Standardization Committees will evaluate tender samples with highest priority as per the direction of circular 14-2021
- As per the consent of the user and the concurrence of the Chairman of the relevant Standardization Committee, the Standardization Committee will accept other samples, subjected to,
  - If they are received only from SLT registered suppliers
  - If they are received only through SLT Supply Chain Management Division with their authorization
- These samples will be given second priority as first priority will always be given to tender samples.
- However, in the event existing contractors propose materials (to be used within the project scope) to be supplied under their contract through an amendment and meeting quality standards and unit prices, it shall be prioritized over above in view effectively utilizing such materials without being a SLT stock item.
- Chairman of the Standardization Committee has the authority to request Supply Chain Management Division to stop the reception of non-tender samples at any time.

K A Kiththi Perera  
Chief Executive Officer
