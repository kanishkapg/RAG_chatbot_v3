CEO's Circular No. 10/2020

CEO's Office
Sri Lanka Telecom PLC
Colombo 01
18 February 2020

To: All SLT Employees

SALARY INCREASE FOR THE YEAR 2020

SLT Management is pleased to inform you that Board of Directors has approved a fixed salary increase of 8.5% for all SLT employees with effect from 1st January 2020. Other conditions will be as per the company's "Annual Salary Revision, Bonus & Leave Encashment Policy". However, employees who have taken unauthorized no-pay leave during the year 2019 will not be entitled for the annual salary increase.

All employees who have joined on contract basis before 1st January 2019 are entitled to receive this benefit based on the terms and conditions of the contract of employment and its renewal.

The management of SLT is grateful on the initiatives taken by all members of the staff in improving productivity and reducing cost during the last year. We look forward to your fullest support in the future as well.

K A Kiththi Pepera
Chief Executive Officer
