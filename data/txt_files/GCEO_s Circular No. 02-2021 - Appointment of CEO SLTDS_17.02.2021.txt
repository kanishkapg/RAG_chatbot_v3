GCEO’s Circular No. 02/02/2021

17th February 2021

To: All Staff,

Circular – Appointment of Mr. Upul Manchanayake as CEO, Designate

The Board of Directors of SLT Digital Info Services have decided to appoint Mr. Upul Manchanayake, General Manager/SLTDS (IT & Operations) to the position of CEO -Designate of SLTDS with effect from 1st of March 2021.

The appointment to the position of CEO/ SLTDS to be taken up by Mr. Manchanayake on the Retirement of Mr. Malraj Balapitiya, CEO/SLTDS.

We wish Mr. Manchanayake all the best on his new appointment.
