CEO’s Office

Colombo 01

To: All SLT Employees

SALARY INCREASE FOR THE YEAR 2019

SLT Management is pleased to inform you the salary increase with relevant to the company’s “Annual Salary Revision, Bonus & Leave Encashment Policy”. The Board of Directors has approved a fixed salary increase of 5.25% and a Performance based salary increment up to a maximum of 2.25% to all employees with effect from 1st January 2019.

The individual performance based salary increment will be paid in March 2019 after finalizing the performance evaluation of the employees for the year 2018 with the arrears of increment effective from 1st January 2019.

All employees who have joined on contract basis before 1st January 2018 are entitled to receive this benefit, whereas others will receive the salary increase with effect from the next date of renewal of their contract.

The management of SLT is grateful for the strong contribution, commitment, hard work and dedication taken by all members of the staff for developing our business, improving productivity and reducing cost during last year. We look forward to your continued cooperation in the future as well.

I wish you and your families a Very Happy and Prosperous New Year!

K A Kitpuk Perera
Chief Executive Officer
