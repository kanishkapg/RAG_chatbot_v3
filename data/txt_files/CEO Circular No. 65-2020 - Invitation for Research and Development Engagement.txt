CEO’s Office, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 01.

To: All SLT Employees,

INVITATION FOR RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT ENGAGEMENT

We are pleased to inform that, SLT has established a “Research and Development Center” under the Corporate and Digital Group. Employees interested in research & innovation activities are invited to join hands with the R&D center. Opportunities are open for all enthusiastic staff members with new ideas to contribute, wherein the R&D center will engage with the aforesaid staff members to play a facilitative role to develop their idea and groom them as innovators. Research & Development has been recognized by the management to play a pivotal role in the success of the company, and employees who contribute in R&D activities will be recognized to develop their career in the industry.

R&D web portal (https://www.sltdigitallab.lk) is introduced, so that Employees will be able to engaged in R&D activities in the following illustrated methods through the aforesaid portal.

1. Idea Funneling

SLT “Intrapreneurship Studio” program is created to support internal innovators and potential entrepreneurs to transform their ideas into groundbreaking new products and solutions. Innovative ideas are welcome from the employees who value collaborative efforts and who are willing to go the extra mile in their career. We will be helping making your idea a success by leveraging SLT’s technologies and expertise. You are mostly welcome to share your innovative ideas and thoughts with the R&D team in respect to the three main R&D areas, as mentioned below.

Your innovative idea is truly valuable to us, even if you contribute only as an idea provider, where such novel idea will be realized by the internally managed talent pool. Please submit your idea via the R&D web portal as given below. Your proposal will be evaluated and the best realized idea producers will be awarded with attractive rewarding schemes.

As the first step, Idea producers are initially encouraged to submit the ideas within two weeks of this invitation, which will be proceeded to the incubator and accelerator stages. Similarly, continuous idea generation is also welcome and such ideas will be funneled to the incubator and accelerator stages periodically.

Idea contributors will be duly recognized and rewarded in numerous ways as a formal career development process in the company.

**Method of Engagement:**

Please refer the SLT R&D Web Portal and submit your concepts, ideas, skill levels and engagement areas within two weeks of this invitation circulation.
