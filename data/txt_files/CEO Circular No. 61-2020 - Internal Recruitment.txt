CEO’s Circular No: £1/2020

CEO’s Office, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 01.

To All SLT Staff,

Internal Recruitment

Applications are invited from SLT employees who have completed three years of permanent confirmed and satisfactory service in SLT, possessing below stated qualifications and fulfill the other eligibility criteria as at 31st October 2020 for the below posts.

Vacancy Positions,

1. Engineer-A5
2. Accountant-A5
3. Telecommunication Technical Officer-A9
4. IT & Network Officer-A9/ Software Developer-A9
5. Marketing Officer-A9

1. Qualification & Eligibility Criteria Required for Each Position.

1.1 Engineer-A5

a) Four-year Degree in BSc Engineering/ Bachelor of Technology (Telecommunication/Electrical & Electronic/ Computer Engineering/ Information Engineering/Electrical) from a University/Institute recognized by UGC and Institute of Engineers, Sri Lanka, equivalent to SLQF 6.
    OR
b) Associate Member of the Institution of Engineers, Sri Lanka.
    AND
c) Three years of permanent, confirmed and satisfactory service in SLT as at the date specified above.

1.2 Accountant-A5

a) Associate Membership of ICA/CIMA/ACCA with 02 years’ post qualifying Finance/Audit experience at SLT as at the date specified above.
    AND
b) Three years of permanent, confirmed and satisfactory service in SLT as at the date specified above.

Note: Four-year full-time Degree in Accountancy/Finance from a University/Institute recognized by UGC-Sri Lanka equivalent to SLQF 6 would be an added advantage.

1.3 Telecommunication Technical Officer-A9

a) G.C.E. (O/L) examination with 06 passes including credit passes in Sinhala/Tamil, English Language & Mathematics in one sitting, and 03 passes at the G.C.E. (A/L) examination in one sitting.
    AND
b) A Higher Diploma in Telecommunication/Electronic/Communication Technology/Computer/ Electrical/Digital Technology/Power/Civil/Air Conditioning, etc. equivalent to National Vocational Qualification 6 (NVQ-6) or SLQF 4 or above from a University/Institute recognized by UGC Sri Lanka or any other government authorities.
    OR
c) Registration at Engineering Council, Sri Lanka as an “Engineering Diplomate” in relevant field.
    AND
d) Three years of permanent, confirmed and satisfactory service in SLT as at the date specified above.

1.4 IT & Network Officer/Software Developer-A9

a) G.C.E. (O/L) examination with 06 passes including credit passes in Sinhala/Tamil, English Language & Mathematics in one sitting, and 03 passes at the G.C.E. (A/L) examination in one sitting.
    AND
b) Higher Diploma in System Design, ACS Diploma, BCS Diploma, IT, Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Software Engineering, Software Development, System Design, Web Designing etc obtained from a recognized institute by UGC-Sri Lanka or any other government authorities, equivalent to NVQ-6 or SLQF 4 or above.
    OR
c) BTEC (IT stream) qualification and 02 years post qualified experience at SLT.
    AND
d) Three years of permanent, confirmed and satisfactory service in SLT as at the date specified above.

1.5 Marketing Officer-A9

a) G.C.E. (O/L) examination with 06 passes having 04 credit passes including Sinhala / Tamil, English Language and Mathematics in one sitting and 03 passes at the G.C.E. (A/L) examination in one sitting.
    AND
b) A Diploma in Marketing from SLIM/CIM Part II or Advanced Diploma in Marketing from a University/Institute recognized by UGC-Sri Lanka or any other government authorities, equivalent to NVQ-6 or SLQF 4 or above.
    AND
c) Three years of permanent, confirmed and satisfactory service in SLT as at the date specified above.

2. Method of Selection

Selection will be made through a written examination (English Medium) and computer based practical test (applicable for IT & Network Officer/Software Developer). Only those who achieve required competency at the examination/test will be called for the interviews for selection. Placement will be done on par with organizational operational priorities.

Subjects specified for the examination are as follows:
(a) Subject Knowledge (2 hours)
i) Multiple Choice Questions (45 Minutes)
ii) Essay Type Questions (1 ¼ hours)
(b) Intelligence Quotient & General Knowledge (1 hour)

3. Method of Applying

A4-size application prepared as per the specimen attached in annexure 1 together with scan copies of relevant certificates should be e-mailed to hr@slt.com.lk through relevant A5 or above officer (with his/her recommendation) to reach SLT HR within 7 working days of issuing this circular.
.
The post/posts applied for should be mentioned in the subject line of the e-mail. Applications sent without supporting documents will not be entertained. Only shortlisted applicants will be contacted by HR.
