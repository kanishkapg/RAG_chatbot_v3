CEO's Circular No. 52/2020

CEO's Office, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 
01 September 2020

To: All Executive Staff,

GUIDELINES ON INCURRING OF EXPENSES

Reference to the Circular number 34/2020 issued in June 2020.

Please note that the discretionary expenses need to be incurred, after seeking prior approval from the Finance division (either from GM Finance, Reporting & Tax – Mr. Indrajith Priyantha or Group CFO).

There will be no restrictions on incurring expenses on urgent repairs and maintenance work or any other work directly related to the Operations.

Further, this restriction is not applicable for petty cash expenses (Opex), where the limit is Rs. 20,000 per transaction. This is applicable till the year end 2020.

Thank you for your co-operation.

K A Kiththi Perera  
Chief Executive Officer
