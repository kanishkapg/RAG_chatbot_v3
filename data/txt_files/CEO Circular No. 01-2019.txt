ENHANCING CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE THROUGH CONSOLIDATION OF NOCS AND ESTABLISHMENT OF SERVICE OPERATION CENTRES (SOC)

In today’s highly competitive Telco market place, service providers need to deliver superior services to their customers. In order to achieve this, SLT need to transit from network operations to service centric operations consolidating all relevant stakeholders under SOC.

Service Operation Centers are formed with the following focus and objectives.

Focus

- Correlations of services
- Performance threshold alerts
- Network element management
- Probing and analyzing available offline data lakes
- Quick actions on service issues and prioritizing workload
- Proactive Service Assurance

Objectives

i. Streamlining the processes
ii. Identifying and Solving the root causes (Reactive and Proactive)
iii. Centralizing the operation in Service Fulfillment and Service Assurance
iv. Multi-skilling the staff
v. Optimizing operational cost (Staff, Vehicles, Space, Etc.)
vi. Improve customer satisfaction
vii. Moving forward with digital services
viii. Data analysis and automation
ix. Central supervision on information security
x. Efficient management on entire vehicle fleet
