ESTABLISHMENT OF TECHNICAL AND COMMERCIAL EVALUATION TEAMS (TCET) AND UPDATE OF EVALUATION TEAM APPOINTMENT FORMS

SLT Management has identified that there is a requirement of establishing new Technical and Commercial Evaluation Teams (TCET) for tender evaluations amalgamating the scope and responsibilities of the existing separate Technical Evaluation Teams (TET) and singular Commercial Evaluation Team (CET) to cater the specific requirements of evaluating both technical and commercial aspect by one team.

In order to facilitate the establishment of TCET, ToR for the TCET was approved by the SLT Board of Directors with the relevant guidelines. All the details relevant to TCET are published in the Intranet under bellow URL.

(Intranet -> Groups -> CSCMO -> Procurement -> Links -> Guidelines)

http://insite.slt.com.lk/sites/cao/procurement/Guide_Lines/Procurement%20Guide%20Lines/ToR%20for%20TCET%20(Technical%20and%20Commercial%20Evaluation%20Team).pdf

In Sole Source Tenders, the Sole Source Evaluation Team (SSET) will be empowered to perform the commercial evaluation for the respective purchase, acting as a special TCET in evaluating a sole source purchase.

Further, in order to complete an evaluation within the reasonable time line agreed with the relevant Sponsor it is noted that there is a requirement of revisiting the Guidelines and Forms that are being used to establish the evaluation teams.

To facilitate this requirement and monitoring of compliance, all evaluation team appointment forms were amended. All amended forms are available under following URL in Intranet.

(Intranet -> Groups -> CSCMO -> Procurement -> Links -> Downloads -> Appointing Members for Evaluation Teams)

http://insite.slt.com.lk/sites/cao/procurement/Downloads/Procurement_Templates/TENDER%20DOCUMENTS/Appointing%20Members%20for%20Evaluation%20Teams

All are expected to use the updated forms for appointment of Evaluation teams w.e.f. \(^{1st}\) March 2021.
