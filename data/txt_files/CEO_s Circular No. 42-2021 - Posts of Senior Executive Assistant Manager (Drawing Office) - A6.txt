POSTS OF SENIOR EXECUTIVE ASSISTANT MANAGER (DRAWING OFFICE) - A6  

Applications are invited from the permanent, confirmed SLT employees who satisfy the under mentioned requirements to be considered for the above mentioned position.  

A. ELIGIBILITY  

Candidates should have completed five (05) years' service as Senior Assistant Manager (Drawing Office) - A7 in SLT as at 31st August 2021  

B. SELECTION METHOD  

1. Selection will be made through an examination and an interview.  
2. Examination will be held in English Medium.  
3. Interview will be held only for successful candidates of the examination for the final selection.  

The subjects specified for the examination are as follows:  
i) SLT Procedures, Practices & Business Affairs (40 minutes)  
ii) Subject Knowledge  
- Part I - Multiple Choice Questions (45 minutes)  
- Part II - Essay Type Questions (1 ¼ hours)  

C. PLACEMENT  

Selected candidates will be promoted as Senior Executive Assistant Manager (Drawing Office) A6 Grade.  

D. METHOD OF APPLYING  

Eligible candidates should fill in the attached application form and forward to GM/ HR & IR, 7th Floor, HQ Building, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Lotus Road, Colombo 01 through the relevant General Manager / Deputy General Manager.  

E. CLOSING DATE  

Applications should be sent within seven (07) working days of issuing this circular. Applications sent after the closing date will not be considered.  

K. A. Kiththi Perera  
Chief Executive Officer
