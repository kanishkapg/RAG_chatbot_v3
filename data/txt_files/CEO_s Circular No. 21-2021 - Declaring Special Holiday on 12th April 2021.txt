CEO's Circular No: 21 /2021

CEO's Office, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 01.

9th April 2021

To All Staff,

DECLARING SPECIAL HOLIDAY ON 12TH APRIL 2021

The Government of Sri Lanka has declared Monday, the 12th of April 2021 as a Special Holiday on account of the Sinhala & Hindu New year 2021.

Therefore, SLT Management has decided to declare a special holiday for all SLT employees on 12th April 2021. However, the employees who have already been assigned operational/essential duties on this day should report for work and overtime payment will be made accordingly.

K A Kitthth Perera Chief Executive Officer
