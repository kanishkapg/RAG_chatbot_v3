CEO's Circular No: 10 /2021

CEO's Office, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 01.

25th February 2021

To All SLT Staff,

AMENDMENT TO CEO'S CIRCULAR NO. 61/2020 ON INTERNAL RECRUITMENT

Qualification requirement for internal recruitment of Telecommunication Technical Officer-A9, IT & Network Officer/Software Developer-A9 and Marketing Officer-A9 positions stated in the CEO's Circular No. 61/2020 dated 25th November 2020 will be amended as follows.

1. Telecommunication Technical Officer-A9,

(a) G.C.E. (O/L) Exam with 06 passes at one sitting with credit passes for Sinhala/Tamil, English Language and Mathematics.
AND
(b) A Higher Diploma in Telecommunication/Electronic/Communication Technology/Computer/ Electrical/ Digital Technology/Power/Civil/Air Conditioning, etc. equivalent to National Vocational Qualification 6 (NVQ-6) or SLQF 4 or above from a University/ Institute recognized by UGC Sri Lanka or any other government authorities.
OR
(c) Registration at Engineering Council, Sri Lanka as an "Engineering Diploma" in relevant field.
AND
(d) Three years of permanent, confirmed and satisfactory service in SLT as at the date specified in the CEO's Circular No. 61/2020.

2. IT & Network Officer/Software Developer-A9,

(a) G.C.E. (O/L) Exam with 06 passes at one sitting with credit passes for Sinhala/Tamil, English Language and Mathematics.
AND
(b) Higher Diploma in System Design, ACS Diploma, BCS Diploma, IT, Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Software Engineering, Software Development, System Design, Web Designing etc, obtained from a UGC-Sri Lanka recognized institute or any other government authorities, equivalent to NVQ-6 or SLQF 4 or above.
OR
(c) BTEC (IT stream) qualification and 02 years post qualified experience at SLT.
AND
(d) Three years of permanent, confirmed and satisfactory service in SLT as at the date specified in the CEO's Circular No. 61/2020.

3. Marketing Officer-A9

(a) Six Subjects in the G.C.E. (O/L) examination in one sitting with four credit passes including Sinhala / Tamil, English Language and Mathematics.

AND

(b) A Diploma in Marketing from SLIM/CIM Part II or Advanced Diploma in Marketing from a University/Institute recognized by UGC-Sri Lanka or any other government authorities, equivalent to NVQ-6 or SLQF 4 or above.

AND

(c) Three years of permanent, confirmed and satisfactory service in SLT as at the date specified in the CEO's Circular No. 61/2020.

Fresh candidates can submit their applications together with scan copies of relevant certificates should e-mail to hr@slt.com.lk through relevant A5 or above officer (with his/her recommendation) to reach DGM-HR Strategies & Policy within 7 working days of issuing this circular.

The 'Position/Positions applied' for should be mentioned in the subject line of the e-mail. Applications sent without supporting documents will not be entertained. Only shortlisted applicants will be contacted by HR.
