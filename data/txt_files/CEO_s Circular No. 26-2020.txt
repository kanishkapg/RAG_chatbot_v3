To All Employees,

Declaring Special Holiday on 04th of June 2020

The government of Sri Lanka has declared Thursday, the 04th of June 2020 as a Holiday for Government institutions due to island wide curfew.

Therefore, SLT Management has decided to declare a special holiday for all SLT employees on 04th of June 2020. However, the employees who have already been assigned operational/essential duties on this day should report for work and Overtime payment will be made accordingly.

K A Kithini Peera  
Chief Executive Officer
