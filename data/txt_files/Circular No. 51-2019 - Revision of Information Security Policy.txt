REVISION OF SLT INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY

The SLT Information Security Policy (Document Number CEO LIS 001 - Issue No 009) has been revised and shall be effective from 9th September 2019.

This document is published in the SLT Common Documents Portal for your reference.

It is the responsibility of all employees to strictly adhere to the contents of this and the associated policies. If any employee fails to comply with these policies outlined, he or she shall be dealt with SLT disciplinary procedure as it might consider reasonable and appropriate.

The objective of issuing this circular is to protect our information that is considered to be a very valuable and a great asset of the company.
