CEO's Circular No: 41/2019

CEO's Office, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 01.

6 August 2019.

To: All Members of Sri Lanka Telecom Provident Fund

RE: INTRODUCTION OF CRITICAL ILLNESS BENEFIT SCHEME TO THE MEMBERS OF SRI LANKA TELECOM PROVIDENT FUND (SLTPF)

We are pleased to inform you that the Management Committee of SLTPF has approved to introduce a Critical Illness Benefit Scheme to the Members of SLTPF with effect from 1st of January 2019.

As per the above approval, all Members of SLTPF who are also permanent employees of SLT are entitled to claim a maximum of Rupees Three Hundred Thousand (Rs 300,000.00) during his or her membership for the Critical Illnesses listed in Annex - 1.

The said Critical Illness Benefit shall be offered in addition to the prevailing death and permanent disability benefit.

The Members, who wish to claim the medical benefit under the above mentioned scheme, should submit a duly filled application (Annex - 2) to The Employee Relation Section (Medical Unit) of SLT. The amount approved will be paid by the Critical Illness Benefit Scheme of Sri Lanka Telecom Provident Fund.

For further clarifications, you may contact following officers of EPF Section.
