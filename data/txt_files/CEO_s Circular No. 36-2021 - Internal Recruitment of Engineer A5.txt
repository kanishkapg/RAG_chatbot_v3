CEO's Circular No: 36/2021

CEO's Office, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 01.

06 August 2021

To All SLT Staff,

Internal Recruitment of Engineer (A5)

Applications are invited from the permanent, confirmed SLT employees who possess the following qualifications for the above post.

1. Eligibility

1.1 Three years of permanent confirmed and satisfactory service in SLT as at 31st July 2021.
AND
1.2 The candidates should possess the following qualifications:

I. Four-year Degree in BSc Engineering/ Bachelor of Technology (Telecommunication/Electrical & Electronic/ Computer Engineering/ Information Engineering/Electrical) from a University/Institute recognized by UGC and Institute of Engineers, Sri Lanka, equivalent to SLQF 6.
OR
II. Associate Member of the Institution of Engineers, Sri Lanka.

2. Method of Selection

Selection will be made through a written examination (English Medium) and interviews.

3. Method of Applying

A4-size application prepared as per the specimen attached in annexure 1 together with scanned copies of relevant certificates should be e-mailed to navomi@slt.com.lk through relevant A5 or above officer (with his/her recommendation) to reach DGM-HR Strategies & Policy within 7 working days of issuing this circular.

"The post applied for" should be mentioned in the subject line of the e-mail. Applications sent without supporting documents will not be entertained. Only shortlisted applicants will be contacted by HR.
