To: All Staff,

DECLARATION OF SLT CUSTOMER SERVICE WEEK 2020

The International Customer Service Association celebrates “International Customer Service Week” every year during the first week of October. Accordingly, they have declared that the week from 05th to 09th October in 2020 as the International Customer Service Week.

Along with that, for the third consecutive year, SLT has planned to celebrate the Customer Service Week (CSW) with the main objective of thanking to all our employees for their unconditional dedication towards delivering a superior customer experience.

Accordingly, the SLT Customer Service Week inaugural ceremony will commence on the 05th October 2020 at SLT Auditorium.

Let us all get together and celebrate the SLT Customer Service Week with the aim of building strong internal relationships with all cross functional teams to deliver the message that Sri Lanka Telecom is truly committed to enhancing customer experience in every milestone throughout the customer journey.

K.A. Kiththi Perera Chief Executive Officer
