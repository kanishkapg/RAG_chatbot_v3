CEO’s Circular No:44/2021

CEO’s Office, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 01.
21 September 2021

To All Security Staff,

POSTS OF TEAM LEADERS (SECURITY), PATROL LEADERS AND AN ASSITANT SECURITY MANAGER

Applications are invited from the permanent, confirmed SLT Security Staff who satisfy the under mentioned requirements to be considered for the above mentioned positions.

A. ELIGIBILITY

Team Leaders (Security) - S4
- 10 years satisfactory service as a Patrolman in the S5 salary grad at SLT a as at 31st August 2021
  AND
  Should have followed at least 05 prescribed training courses conducted by SLT Security Division whilst service as Patrolman

Patrol Leaders - S3
- 06 years satisfactory service as a Team Leader (Security) in the S4 salary grade at SLT as at 31st August 2021
  AND
  Should have followed at least 03 prescribed training courses conducted by SLT Security Division whilst service as Team Leader (Security)

Assistant Security Manager - S1.1
- 05 years satisfactory service as a Security Officer in the S2 Grade at SLT as at 31st August 2021

B. SELECTION METHOD
Selection will be made through an interview.

C. PLACEMENT
Selected candidates will be promoted as Assistant Security Manager-S1.1/Team Leader (Security) - S4 / Petrol Leader – S3 and placed under vacant positions

D. METHOD OF APPLYING
Eligible candidates should fill in the attached application form and forward to GM/ HR & IR 7th Floor, HQ Building, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Lotus Road, Colombo 01 through the relevant General Manager / Deputy General Manager.

E. CLOSING DATE
Applications should be sent within seven (07) working days of issuing this circular. Applications sent after the closing date will not be considered.

K. A. Kjhihi-Perera
Chief Executive Officer
