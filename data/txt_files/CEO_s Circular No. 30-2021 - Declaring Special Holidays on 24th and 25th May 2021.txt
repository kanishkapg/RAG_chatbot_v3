To All Employees,  

DECLARING SPECIAL HOLIDAYS ON 24TH AND 25TH MAY 2021  

The Government of Sri Lanka has declared 24th and 25th of May 2021 as two special holidays for Government offices, to make the efforts of the Government to minimize the movement in the country a success and in order to minimize the rapid spread of Covid 19.  

Hence, SLT Management has decided to declare 24th and 25th of May 2021 as two special holidays for all SLT employees. However, the employees who have already been assigned operational/essential duties on above days should report for work and relevant payments will be made accordingly.
