GCEO’s Circular No: 03/2021

GCEO’s Office, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 01.

30th June 2021

To All Staff,

APPOINTMENT OF GROUP CHIEF SECURITY OFFICER

I’m pleased to inform that Major General Nirmal Ajith Dharmaratne (Retired) will be appointed as the Group Chief Security Officer (GCSO), of SLT PLC with effect from 1st July 2021. He will be overall responsible for the management of Physical Security and Safety in the entire Group and across all locations. In addition he will closely coordinate with respective Cyber Security related Divisions of the Group in order to ensure overall security. GCSO shall report directly to the Group Chief Executive Officer of SLT PLC.

Major General Nirmal Ajith Dharmaratne (Retired) served the Sri Lanka Army from January 1985 to April 2020 and held different positions including Deputy Chief of Staff, Chief of Staff - Office of Chief of Defense Staff, Director General - Office of Chief of Defense Staff, Director of Personnel Administration, Director of Operations and Defense Advisor of Sri Lanka High Commission - UK. Further, Major General Dharmaratne (Retired) served in positions of Commanding Officer of General Sir John Kotelawala Defence University, Commanding Officer of Army Training School and as the Chief Instructor of the Special Forces Training School as well.

Major General Dharmaratne (Retired) holds Master’s Degree in National Resource Strategy from the National Defense University – USA, Master’s Degree in Defense Studies from the University of Kelaniya - Sri Lanka and Diploma in Human Resources Management from CIPM Sri Lanka.

On behalf of SLT Group, I request your fullest corporation to carry out his duties and wish him all success in the new job role.

Lalith Seneviratne
Group Chief Executive Officer
