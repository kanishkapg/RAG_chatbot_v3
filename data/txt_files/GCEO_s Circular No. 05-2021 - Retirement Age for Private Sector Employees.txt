Retirement Age for Private Sector Employees

In relation to the current proposal by the Government to extend the employment retirement age to
60 years, approval for the same to be debated in Parliament of Sri Lanka and related agencies.
Whether this statute will become accepted or not SLT-Mobitel recognizes the strength of its
employees and their experience as an asset to the Company.

We hereby reiterate that we will adhere to any statute passed by the Parliament of Sri Lanka in
accordance with the Sri Lankan Labor Law with regard to the retirement age for private sector
employees.

Lalith Seneviratne

Group Chief Executive Officer
