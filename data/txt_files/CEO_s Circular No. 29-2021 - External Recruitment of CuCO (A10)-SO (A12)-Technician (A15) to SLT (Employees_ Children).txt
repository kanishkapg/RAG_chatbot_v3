EXTERNAL RECRUITMENT OF CUSTOMER CARE OFFICER (A10), SALES OFFICER (A12) AND TECHNICIAN (A15) TO SLT (OPEN ONLY FOR SLT EMPLOYEES' CHILDREN).

Applications are invited from children of SLT employees who are possessing below stated qualifications and fulfill the other eligibility criteria as at 30th April 2021 to be considered for vacancies stated above.

1. Qualification & Eligibility Criteria for the post of Customer Care Officer-A10

a) G.C.E. (O/L) examination or equivalent with six subjects in one sitting with four (04) credit passes including Arithmetic/Mathematics and Sinhala or Tamil Language/Literature and English Language/Literature.  
   AND  
b) Three Passes in GCE (A/L) or equivalent in one sitting.  
   AND  
c) Successful completion of an IT course with at least one-year duration at a recognized institution (having TVEC or Business Registration) with thorough knowledge of MS Office.  

d) Upper Age Limit is 30 Years.

2. Qualification & Eligibility Criteria for the post of Sales Officer-A12.

a) G.C.E. (O/L) six subjects in not more than two sittings with four credit passes including ordinary passes in Arithmetic/Mathematics and Sinhala or Tamil language/Literature and credit pass in English language/Literature.  
   AND  
b) Passed G.C.E. (A/L).  
   AND  
c) Certificate in marketing equivalent to NVQ Level 5 OR a one-year full time Diploma in Marketing (with minimum 60 credits) from a recognized institution (having TVEC or Business Registration).  

d) Upper Age Limit is 35 Years.

3. Qualification & Eligibility Criteria for the post of Technician-A15.

a) 06 passes at the G.C.E. (O/L) exam including Sinhala/ Tamil and English Language and Mathematics and 03 credit passes in one sitting.  
   AND

b) Should have obtained Skilled Competence Certificate-NAITA in the relevant field equivalent to NVQ Level 4 (Telecommunication / Electrical / Power / Air Conditioning etc). OR  
c) Registration at Engineering Council, Sri Lanka as an "Engineering Technician". AND  
d) Possess a valid driving license to drive minimum "B" vehicle class motor vehicles.  
e) Upper Age Limit is 35 Years.  

4. Criteria and other conditions applicable to employees to make eligible their children to the scheme.

a) SLT Permanent employees who are currently working in SLT and having more than 10 years of confirmed and satisfactory service in SLT as at \(30^{th}\) April 2021.  
b) SLT Permanent retired employees who were working in SLT during last 10 years as at \(30^{th}\) April 2021 and having completed more than 10 years of confirmed and satisfactory service in SLT at the date of retirement.  
c) The benefit will only be given to one child of SLT employee. Employees whose child/children were recruited under the previous "employees' children scheme" are not eligible to the scheme.  
d) When both husband & wife (parents of applicant) are employees/retired employee(s) in SLT, only one child is eligible to the scheme.  

5. Method of Selection.  

Selection will be made through a Trade Test/Examination. Only those who achieve required competency at the Trade Test/Examination will be called for an interview for final selection. Placement will be done on par with organization's operational priorities.  

6. Method of Applying  

An application prepared as per the specimen attached in Annexure 1 together with scan copies of relevant certificates should be e-mailed to hr@slt.com.lk to reach DGM-HR Strategies & Policy, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC within 7 working days of issuing this circular.  

The 'post applied' for should be mentioned in the subject line of the e-mail. Applications sent without supporting documents will not be entertained. Only shortlisted applicants will be contacted by HR department.
