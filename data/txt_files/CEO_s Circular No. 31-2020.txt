ESTABLISHMENT OF AN OPERATIONAL EXCELLENCE TASK FORCE (OETF)

The immediate requirement of improving our operational policies, processes, procedures and practices for achieving operational excellence to adapt SLT to the "new normal" of post-COVID-19, and to ensure an excellent customer experience is delivered across all our customer segments has been identified.

In order to facilitate this, an Operational Excellence Task Force (OETF) fully authorized to identify and implement short-term and long-term actions for improving our operational performance has been established with immediate effect. The OETF is composed of the following members and will report to a Steering Committee which includes CEO, COO, CMO, CCDO, CNO, CIO, CSROI, CSROII, CEWO & CCuO

1. Y. L. Ganearachchi (DGM/VAS & CA PL) – Team Leader
2. S. L. R. Paranawithana (DGM/CEM) – Secretary
3. A. D. T. R. Thilakaratine (DGM/CCD)
4. A. S. B. Amunugama (DGM/OS)
5. I. G. B. Wimalasuriya (DGM/BPR)
6. M. C. Wijayaratne (DGM/SFC)
7. R. N. W. Jayawardhana (DGM/WP(SW))
8. T. S. D. Peiris (DGM/IT ST)

I expect your fullest support and cooperation to the OETF by providing requested information, actively participating at OETF meetings when invited and implementing the recommendations of OETF.
