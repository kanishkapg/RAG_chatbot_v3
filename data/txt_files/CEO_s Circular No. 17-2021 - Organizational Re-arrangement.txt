CEO's Circular No: 17/2021

CEO's Office, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 01.

3 March 2021

To All Staff,

ORGANIZATIONAL RE-ARRANGEMENT

Mrs. B. Maneesha Jinadasa who functions as the Chief Supply Chain Management Officer (CSCMO) is due to retire in April 2021. Accordingly, the Supply Chain Management and Commercial Evaluation functions will be structured under the Administrative Group and the following organizational re-arrangement will be implemented with immediate effect;

| Name    | Designation    | Present Reporting Structure | New Reporting Structure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mr. R M S K Piyarathna   | DCSCMO & GM-Commercial Evaluation | CSCMO    | CAO    |
| Mr. W M U I Gunawardana | GM-Supply Chain Management    | CSCMO    | CAO    |

During the transition period Mrs. Jinadasa will work in coordination with Mr. H. K. S. K. Abeysekara (CAO) to affect a smooth handover of functions.

I take this opportunity to thank Mrs. B. M. Jinadasa for her contributions during the tenure of service at Sri Lanka Telecom PLC.

K A Kihtibi Perera
Chief Executive Officer
