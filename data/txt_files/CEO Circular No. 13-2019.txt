CEO’s Circular No: 13 /2019

CEO’s Office, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 
01 March 2019

To All Staff,

POSTS OF SENIOR EXECUTIVE ASSISTANT MANAGER (CONTACT CENTER) – A6

| SN | Vacant Position    |
|---|---|
| 1   | SEAM – Contact Center Bandarawela    |
| 2   | SEAM – Contact Center Kandy    |
| 3   | SEAM – Contact Center Colombo    |

Applications are invited from the permanent, confirmed SLT employees who satisfy the under mentioned requirements to be considered for the above-mentioned vacant positions.

A. ELIGIBILITY
Candidates should have completed five (05) years’ service as a Senior Assistant Manager (Contact Center) - A7 in SLT as at 15th February 2019.

B. SELECTION METHOD
1. Selection will be made through an examination and an interview.
2. Examination will be held in English Medium.
3. Interview will be held only for successful candidates of the examination for the final selection.

The subjects specified for the examination are as follows:
i) SLT Procedures, Practices & Business Affairs (40 minutes)
ii) Subject Knowledge
• Part I - Multiple Choice Questions (45 minutes)
• Part II - Essay Type Questions (1 X hours)

C. PLACEMENT
Selected candidates will be promoted as Senior Executive Assistant Manager (Contact Center) - A6 Grade and placed in the above vacant positions. No transfers will be given until the officer completes four (04) years of service in deployed positions.

D. METHOD OF APPLYING
Eligible candidates should fill in the attached application form and forward to DGM/ HR Operations, 7th Floor, HQ Building, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Lotus Road, Colombo 01 through the relevant General Manager / Deputy General Manager.

E. CLOSING DATE
Applications should be sent within seven (07) working days of issuing this circular. Applications sent after the closing date will not be considered.

K. A. Kiththl-Pérera
Chief Executive Officer
