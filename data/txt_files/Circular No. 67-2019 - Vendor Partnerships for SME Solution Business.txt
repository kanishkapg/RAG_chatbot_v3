VENDER PARTNERSHIPS FOR SME SOLUTION BUSINESS

Given SLT’s unique position with island wide fiber network, well dispersed regional structure with regional enterprise sales staff, we decided to aggressively grow SME business with a major solution business drive in next 03 years.

For this purpose, SLT partnered with SME solution providers who can be utilized to develop tailor-made solutions for our SME customers. Regional Enterprise Business Teams can now engage SME solution partners for designing, developing and delivering business solutions for customers. Partnership operational process is attached herewith.

- Annex 1 : Business process for outright SME Sales
- Annex 2 : Business process for subsidized SME Sales

List of contracted SME Solution Partners with their respective category of solutions is published in the Intranet. Please refer Intranet > Groups > CMO > SME Business Development > Business Guidelines > SME Solution Partners

In this process Regional Committee is authorized to invite bids and recommend the designed business solutions with its solution partners. Guidelines on selecting partners & partnership operational process are also attached;

- Annex 3 : Single SME Partner Approval
- Annex 4 : User Acceptance

High level business process of providing SME solutions with SME solution partners is given below;

- Solution design is to be performed by SLT Regional Enterprise Business (REB) Team together with Selected Solution Partner
- Business proposals will be routed through central SME Solutions Section for the approval.
- Relevant REB Team to handover the business proposal to the customer
- Relevant Technical and Commercial Negotiations are to be conducted in consultation with SME Solution team

Page 1 of 2

- Once the proposal is accepted, the respective REB Manager should register services in MINT and accept due payments via SLT cashiering systems. If solution is offered in OPEX model, required to follow guidelines of CEO/COO circular No 01/2019 on approving subsidized SME Solution sales
- SME Solution Team to handle subsequent additional contract signing, procurement with SME Solution Partner
- REB Team to conduct User Acceptance Test (UAT) at the completion of the job
- SME Solution team to handle partner payments upon the receipt of UAT documents
- Subsequent customer support to be handle by the SME Solution Partner as per the Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC)
- SME Standardization Committee will act as the governance body of SME Solution Partnership Operation. Please refer **Intranet > Groups > CMO > SME Business Development > Business Guidelines > SME Standardization Committee**

The objective of this circular is to regularize operations of SME Solution Partnership Process in view of developing the SME business aggressively in next 03 years.

K A Kiththi Perera  
Chief Executive Officer
