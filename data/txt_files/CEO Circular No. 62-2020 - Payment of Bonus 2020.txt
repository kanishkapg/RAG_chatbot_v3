CEO’s Circular No. 62/2020

CEO’s Office, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 01.

1st December 2020

To: All SLT Employees,

PAYMENT OF BONUS - 2020

SLT Management is pleased to acknowledge and recognize your commitment to maintain our services in this difficult period. As one team, we quickly reacted to the crisis situation and continue to provide an essential service to the country.

Considering your contributions during the pandemic period, the Board of Directors have approved an annual bonus equal to 04 months basic salary for the year 2020, subject to setting off the payment of cash advance paid in April 2020 by SLT.

Payment will be done as shown below;

I. SLT Permanent Employees recruited before the year 2020
Total Bonus of 4 months’ basic salary subject to a minimum of LKR 150,000/-.
II. SLT Permanent Employees recruited during the year 2020
a) Employees who joined SLT between 01.01.2020 - 31.10.2020 will receive a bonus of 4 months’ basic salary subject to a minimum of LKR 150,000/- on prorate basis. However, employees who resigned from HCS and joined SLT from 1st June 2020 onwards will receive a bonus of 4 months basic salary on prorate basis subject to a minimum of Rs. 100,000/-.
b) Employees who joined SLT after 01.11.2020 are not entitled to annual bonus.
III. Contract Basis Employees
An ex-gratia payment will be paid following the similar criteria of payment of bonus to SLT permanent employees under the section I & II above.

The bonus will be paid on or before 10th December 2020 and amount paid as a bonus in December 2020 will be the net bonus after deducting the cash advance payment of 3/4 quarter month basic salary subject to a minimum of LKR 50,000/- paid by SLT in April 2020. All payments will be made in accordance with the bonus payment policy of the company.

I take this opportunity to thank all of you for your significant contribution and dedication during this year. The year ahead will bring its own challenges and opportunities. I am sure with your continuous support and by working together on our priorities and keeping our customers first, we can realize our corporate objectives in the year 2021 as well.

I wish all SLT employees and members of their families a happy & prosperous new year.

K A Kiththi Perera-
Chief Executive Officer
