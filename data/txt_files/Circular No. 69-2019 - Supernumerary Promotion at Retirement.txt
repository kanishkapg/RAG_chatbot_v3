Supernumerary Promotion to Recognize Employees at the Date of Retirement

Please be informed that as a part of service appreciation, the SLT Management has taken appropriate action to offer a Supernumerary promotion of next position / next grade at the date of retirement to exceptionally performing employees with more than 30 years of experience, immense knowledge, expertise and loyalty to SLT PLC.

Employee should have qualified with following pre-requisite conditions to be eligible for the supernumerary promotion at the date of retirement.

Pre-requisite conditions and eligibility criteria

a. More than 30 years of service in SLT PLC (i.e. Contract/Casual period is also considered)  
b. Stagnated more than 10 years at any grade during the career in SLT PLC  
c. Immediate last five years Performance should be above Very Good (V)/ Exceeds Expectation (4)  
d. No disciplinary action, investigation during the service period and no unauthorized no pay during the last 10 years of service.

Supernumerary promotion related salary increment will be offered to successful employees at the date of his/her retirement.

Employees can use attached Annexure 01 to request supernumerary promotion upon their retirement and the request should be forward to DGM/HR Operations three (03) months prior to the date of his / her retirement.
