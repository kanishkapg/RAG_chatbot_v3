OPPORTUNITIES TO BE TECHNICIAN-A15

Employees who are qualified with NVQ Level 4 get an opportunity to apply for the posts of Technician A15 under this special opportunity. Accordingly, applications are invited from permanent SLT employees who possess following qualifications and fulfill other eligibility criteria required for the post of Technician A15.

Qualifications & eligibility criteria:

a) A16 or A15 grade permanent employee with satisfactory service records.
    And
b) 06 passes at the G.C.E. (O/L) exam including Sinhala/ Tamil, English Language and Mathematics and 03 credit passes in one sitting.
    And
c) Should have obtained NVQ Level 4 Certification in the field of Telecommunication / Electrical /IT/ Power /Air Conditioning etc.
    And
d) Possess a valid driving license to drive minimum “B” vehicle class motor vehicles.

Applicants who possess aforementioned qualifications and fulfill other eligibility criteria will be posted as Technician-A15. Applicants who do not have the qualification stated under item “b” above and fulfill other eligibility criteria also can apply. They will be posted as Technician A15 upon successful completion of a special training course followed by an assessment test which will be conducted by SLT Talent Development Centre. Posting will be done after an interview based on the company business requirement.

Method of Applying:
A4-size application prepared as per the specimen attached in annexure 1 together with scan copies of relevant certificates (NVQ 4 certificate, GCE O/L certificate and copy of Driving License) should be e-mailed to hr@slt.com.lk reach SLT HR within 7 working days of issuing this circular.

The post applied for should be mentioned in the subject line of the e-mail. Applications sent without supporting documents will not be entertained. Only shortlisted applicants will be contacted by HR.

K A Kiththj-Perera Chief Executive Officer
