CEO's Circular 23/2020

Chief Executive Officers' Office,
Sri Lanka Telecom PLC,
Colombo 01.
17th May 2020

To: All employees of SLT,

GUIDELINES ON RESUMPTION OF OPERATIONS IN SLT HEADQUARTER
(APPLICABLE FOR WEEK 02 FROM 18TH TO 24TH MAY 2020)

I sincerely thank you for your support, dedication and commitment amidst great personal
difficulties to continue SLT's business operations, since the start of Sri Lanka's response to the
COVID -19 pandemic.

SLT started resumption of its activities in a limited scale last week following the national
guidelines and I appreciate the positive and supportive response received from employees for our
team effort. We are continuing our 'Back to the Normalcy' drive for the 2nd week and all
employees are herewith requested to strictly adhere to the guidelines issued by the Government
and Helath Authorities with respect to the prevention of COVID-19.

Further to CEO's Circular 22/2020, the following guidelines are issued;

1. Working time starts at 10:00 am for all staff.

2. Check the temperature of all the staff at the time of entrance to the workplace and any person
recording a temperature above 98.4 0 F or 37 0 C should be rechecked in 10-15 minutes and
if positive for second time the employee shell be safely sent back home and keep under
observation.

Please extend your support and cooperation.

K A Kihthi Perera
Chief Executive Officer
