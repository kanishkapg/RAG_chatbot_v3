Chairman's Circular No: 04/2019

Chairman's Office
Sri Lanka Telecom PLC
Colombo 01
29.03.2019

To All Employees

**Payment of Advance Bonus based on the performance for Year 2019**

You are pleased to inform that the Board of directors of SLT has approved the payment of
one and quarter months' salary ( 1¼) ( the quarter month's salary is subjected to minimum of
LKR 25,000/-) to all SLT employees as an advance bonus based on the performance of year
2019 in recognizing your commitment and dedication towards achieving the Corporate Goals
during the period from January to March 31, 2019.

This bonus will be paid as an advance bonus for the year 2019 and will be adjusted with the
total amount of bonus at the end of the year. All payments will be made in accordance with the
Bonus Payment Policy of the company.

The Management profoundly appreciate your contribution towards achieving Company's
Goals for Excellence in year 2019 and the bonus proposed to be paid in year 2019 will be
adjusted with the payments made time to time from the cumulative amount.

Further, as per my circular No: 02/2019, the above mentioned minimum payment of LKR
25,000/- will be adjusted with the weighted salary index based on the performance of the year
2019. Due arrangements have been made to pay the above bonus on 4th April 2019.

I wish all SLT employees and members of their families a happy and prosperous Sinhala and
Hindu New Year.

**P.G. Kumarasinghe Sirisena.**
**Chairman**
