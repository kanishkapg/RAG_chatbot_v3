CEO's Circular No. 29/2020

CEO's Office, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 01.

17 June 2020

To All Staff,

USE OF COMPANY ASSETS FOR UPCOMING GENERAL ELECTION - 2020

Management hereby advises all staff members of SLT PLC to refrain from engaging in political activities in favour of any political party or candidate during the upcoming General Election 2020 utilizing company resources, Company name or logo.

The Management stresses that the Company name, logo, physical assets such as vehicles, equipment, financial assets, belonging to the Company should not be utilized for any political activity in support of any political party or candidate during the forthcoming General Elections. Further the Management advises the staff members to refrain from misusing their official title or authority for such purposes.

Further, disciplinary actions will be strictly enforced against any staff member violating the above directions.

K A Kiththi-Perera  
Chief Executive Officer
