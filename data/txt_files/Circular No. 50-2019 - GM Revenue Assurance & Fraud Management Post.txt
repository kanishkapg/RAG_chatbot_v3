CEO's Circular No: 5O/2019

CEO's Office, Sri Lanka Telecom PLC, Colombo 01.
29th August 2019

To All General Managers, Deputy General Managers,

POST OF GM / REVENUE ASSURANCE & FRAUD MANAGEMENT

Applications are invited from the permanent Deputy General Managers of A2, A3 and A4 grades of SLT, who satisfy the under mentioned requirements to be considered for the above post.

1. ELIGIBILITY

1.1 FOR OFFICERS PRESENTLY IN A3 GRADE:

(a) Engineering Profession

(i) Corporate Membership of IESL/IEE(UK)/I Mech E(UK)/Institution of Civil Engineers (UK) with 5 years experience in A3 Grade in SLT

1.2 FOR OFFICERS PRESENTLY IN A4 GRADE:

(a) Engineering Profession

(i) Corporate Membership of IESL/IEE(UK)/I Mech E(UK)/Institution of Civil Engineers(UK) with 3 years engineering experience in A4 Grade in SLT OR
(ii) An Engineering Degree/Associate Membership of IESL/IEE(UK)/I Mech E(UK)/Institution of Civil Engineers(UK) with 5 years engineering experience in A4 Grade in SLT OR
(iii) 7 years engineering experience in A4 Grade in SLT

1.3 Deputy General Managers in A3 Grade who do not possess required number of years experience in A3 Grade under category 1.1 will be eligible under category 1.2 by adding the number of years experience in A4 Grade and A3 Grade together and those officers may also apply.

2. OPPORTUNITY FOR PRESENT GMs & DGMs IN AZ GRADE
GMs (A2/A3) and DGMs (A2) who wish to be considered for the above position may also apply.

3. SELECTION METHOD
Selection will be by an interview.

4. PLACEMENT
After fulfilling the selection criteria of Executive Staff Recruitment & Promotion Scheme (SRPS) and successful completion of the trial period, if the selected officer is in A3 Grade he/she will be placed in A2 Grade and if the selected officer is in A4 Grade he/she will be placed in A3 Grade. Further, if the selected officer is in A2 Grade, his/her Grade will remain unchanged.

5. METHOD OF APPLYING
Eligible candidates should fill in the attached application form and forward to DGM/HR Operations through the relevant Supervising Officer.

6. CLOSING DATE
Applications should be sent within 07 days of issuing this circular.

K. A. Kibthi Perera
Chief Executive Officer
