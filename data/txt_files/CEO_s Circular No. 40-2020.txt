ORGANIZATION TRANSFORMATION – NEW ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE FOR SLT PLC

New Organizational Structure is proposed with necessary changes for digital transformation and market exploration to become the most preferred digital life style provider of Sri Lanka with global presence.

The new organizational structure change will impact mostly to achieve, the target business growth over and above the expectation levels. And this organizational structure will enable below aspects;

1. To fast track the digital transformation.
2. To strengthen the Research & Development within the SLT.
3. To establish a separate section where priority is given to health & safety of SLT employees.
